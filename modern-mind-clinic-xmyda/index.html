Location

Modern Mind Clinic is a dedicated mental health practice located at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075. Our clinic is founded on the principle of providing advanced, evidence-based psychiatric treatments in a modern and supportive environment. We understand that the journey to mental wellness is unique for each individual, and our mission is to offer specialized therapeutic options for those who have not found sufficient relief with traditional methods. At our Southfield clinic, we focus on cutting-edge interventions designed to foster profound and lasting change.

The cornerstone of our practice at the Southfield location is Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation, or TMS Therapy. This is a non-invasive, FDA-cleared treatment that uses targeted magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain regions involved in mood regulation. For individuals struggling with Major Depressive Disorder, TMS offers a promising alternative. The treatment is conducted while you are awake and alert, in a comfortable chair at our clinic. There are no systemic side effects like those often associated with medication, and no recovery time is needed, allowing you to resume your daily activities immediately after each session at our Southfield office.

In addition to TMS, Modern Mind Clinic provides Spravato (esketamine) nasal spray treatment. Spravato is an FDA-approved medication for Treatment-Resistant Depression, administered under the direct supervision of our clinical team right here in Suite 750. This treatment represents a significant advancement in psychiatric care, offering a new mechanism of action for patients who need a different approach. Each Spravato session is conducted in a private, calming space within our clinic, where your safety and comfort are our top priorities from start to finish.

Understanding the need for accessible and continuous care, Modern Mind Clinic integrates telehealth services into our practice. This allows us to provide consistent support and psychiatric management to our patients from the convenience of their own homes, while maintaining a strong connection to our home base at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd. Our telehealth platform ensures that follow-up appointments, medication management, and therapeutic consultations are seamlessly integrated with the in-clinic treatments we offer, creating a comprehensive and modern care model.

Every treatment plan at Modern Mind Clinic is personalized. We begin with a thorough assessment at our Southfield location to understand your specific history, challenges, and goals. Our team is committed to guiding you through every step of the process, from the initial consultation to the completion of your treatment regimen. We believe in a collaborative approach, working with you to design a path toward improved mental wellness that aligns with your life. If you are seeking a modern solution for depression, we invite you to contact our clinic to learn more.

FAQ

1. What is TMS Therapy and where is it performed?

TMS Therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. All TMS treatments are administered in-person at our clinical office located at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075.

2. How do I schedule an appointment at the Modern Mind Clinic?

Appointments for consultations, TMS, and Spravato treatments are scheduled directly through our Southfield clinic. You can contact us by phone at (248) 607-7190 or through the contact form on our website, https://www.modernmindclinic.com/.

3. Are the telehealth services connected to your physical clinic?

Yes, our telehealth services are an extension of the care provided at our Southfield location. They are designed for follow-ups and management, ensuring continuous support from the same clinical team based at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750.

4. Is Spravato treatment done at the clinic?

Yes, Spravato treatment is exclusively administered on-site at our Southfield clinic. Patients must come to our office at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750 for each session, where they are monitored by our medical staff in a dedicated treatment room.

5. Do I need a referral to be seen at your Southfield location?

While a referral from your current provider can be helpful, it is not mandatory. You can contact Modern Mind Clinic directly at (248) 607-7190 to schedule an initial evaluation at our Southfield office to determine if our treatments are right for you.
