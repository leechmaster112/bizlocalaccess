Modern Mind Clinic specializes in Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) Therapy, an innovative, non-invasive treatment for depression. TMS is a safe and effective alternative for individuals who have struggled with medication-resistant depression. Spravato is another FDA-approved medication for treatment-resistant depression, offering a new mechanism of action for patients who need a different approach. FAQ

1. What is TMS Therapy and how is it administered at your Southfield clinic?

TMS Therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain. At our Modern Mind Clinic location at 18000 W Nine Mile Rd Suite 750, Southfield, MI 48075, you will sit in a comfortable chair while a magnetic coil is placed gently against your scalp. The treatment sessions typically last around 20-40 minutes, and you can return to your normal routine immediately afterward.

2. Is Spravato treatment done in-person at the Southfield office?

Yes, Spravato treatment is administered on-site at our Southfield clinic.
