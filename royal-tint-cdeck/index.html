FAQ

1. What types of window tint do you offer at your Rocklin location?

At our facility on 5600 Pacific St, we offer a range of high-quality ceramic, carbon, and dyed window films. Ceramic tint provides the highest level of heat rejection and UV protection without interfering with electronic signals, making it a popular choice for modern vehicles.

2. How long does a typical window tint installation take at your shop?

The duration depends on the vehicle and the type of film, but most standard cars are completed within a few hours at our Rocklin service center. We ask that you plan for half a day to ensure the film has proper time to be installed and set correctly before you drive away.

3. Can paint protection film be applied to any part of the vehicle?

Yes, our specialists at the Rocklin location can apply paint protection film to any exterior surface. The most common applications are full front ends (bumper, hood, fenders, mirrors), headlights, rocker panels, and door edges to protect against the most common road hazards.

4. How do I care for my window tint after installation?

We provide all clients at our 5600 Pacific St location with specific aftercare instructions. Generally, we recommend avoiding rolling down windows and cleaning the glass for about one week after installation. After that, use a soft, clean cloth and ammonia-free cleaner to maintain the film's clarity and finish.

5. Do I need an appointment for services at Royal Tint?

Yes, we highly recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure we can dedicate the necessary time and attention to your vehicle. You can book your consultation or service by calling us directly at 916-840-0030.
