Artevino Wine

Artevino Wine is a premier artisanal winery nestled in the Yorkville Highlands of Anderson Valley, specializing in hand-crafted natural wines that showcase the pure essence of Mendocino County terroir. Located at 20799 CA-128 in Yorkville, California, this small-production winery is dedicated to sustainable, earth-friendly winemaking practices that prioritize quality and authenticity over mass production. Under the expert guidance of third-generation fruit farmer and winemaker Tom Rodrigues, Artevino produces award-winning Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Merlot, and exceptional Dessert Wines, all crafted with zero pesticides, zero herbicides, and absolutely no artificial additives, colorings, or flavorings that plague conventional wines. What sets Artevino apart is their unwavering commitment to natural winemaking with minimal intervention. While conventional winemakers can legally use over 60 additives to mask inferior fruit, Artevino takes the opposite approach: letting Mother Nature and superior vineyard practices do the work. Their organically and sustainably grown grapes benefit from the ideal growing conditions of the Yorkville Highlands—warm days, cool nights, and coastal fog, creating wines with remarkable character and complexity. Each harvest is personally overseen by Tom Rodrigues, who learned from his grandfather that "get your fruit ripe and the rest is easy," a philosophy that guides every bottle produced. Whether you're seeking natural wines for everyday enjoyment or exploring their wine club membership options, Artevino Wine offers an authentic tasting experience that proves natural wine simply tastes better. Visit their tasting room to discover why discerning wine lovers throughout Yorkville and beyond consider Artevino the destination for pure, artisanal natural wines.
