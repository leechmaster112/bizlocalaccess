Boat From Miami to Key West

Located at 777 Brickell Ave #500, Miami, FL 33131, Boat From Miami to Key West offers exceptional boat charters departing exclusively from our Miami headquarters. We specialize in providing unforgettable experiences sailing from the vibrant shores of Miami to the charming island of Key West.

Our carefully curated fleet ensures a premium experience for both day trips and private charters. Each journey offers breathtaking views of the turquoise waters, vibrant coral reefs, and the unique Florida Keys ecosystem. We pride ourselves on combining state-of-the-art vessels with professional captains who know the optimal routes for the best scenery and marine life encounters.

Our Services:

Day Trips: Perfect for experiencing the journey from Miami to Key West. These excursions typically last 4-5 hours and include all necessary amenities for a comfortable day out on the water.

Private Charters: Customized experiences for groups, special occasions, or corporate events. We accommodate parties of all sizes with personalized service and attention to detail.

At our Miami base, we handle all logistics including vessel preparation, safety briefings, and ensuring a seamless experience from departure to return. Our commitment to environmental responsibility means we adhere strictly to Florida's marine regulations and promote sustainable tourism practices.
