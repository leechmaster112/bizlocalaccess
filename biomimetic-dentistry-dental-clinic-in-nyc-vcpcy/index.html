Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly is biomimetic dentistry?

A1: Biomimetic dentistry focuses on restoring teeth in a way that mimics their natural structure and function. This means using conservative techniques, preserving as much healthy tooth as possible, and selecting materials that integrate well and provide strength, much like your natural tooth.

Q2: Do you only use tooth-colored materials?

A2: While we utilize advanced tooth-colored materials like composite and porcelain for aesthetic restorations, we also employ proven materials like gold and sometimes ceramic for crowns, depending on the specific needs, function, and longevity required for each case, always prioritizing the best biomimetic outcome.

Q3: How does biomimetic dentistry help with teeth grinding?

A3: For bruxism (teeth grinding), we create custom-fitted night guards using durable biomimetic materials. These guards are designed to protect your teeth from excessive forces, distribute pressure evenly, and mimic the natural cushioning effect of the jaw joint and surrounding tissues.

Q4: Is biomimetic dentistry more expensive than traditional dentistry?

A4: The cost varies depending on the specific procedure, materials chosen, and the extent of the restoration needed. While sometimes the initial investment might be higher due to advanced materials and techniques, the focus is on long-lasting results, which can often lead to fewer future treatments and greater overall value.

Q5: What should I expect during a biomimetic consultation?

A5: During your consultation at our 115 E 61st St #14A practice, we will listen to your concerns and goals. We'll perform a thorough examination, possibly including X-rays, discuss your options focusing on conservative and long-lasting solutions, and explain the biomimetic approach to ensure you understand the recommended treatment plan.
