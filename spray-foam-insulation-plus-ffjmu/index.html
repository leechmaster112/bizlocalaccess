Our Core Service: Spray Foam Insulation

Our primary service revolves around the application of spray foam insulation. This advanced material is distinguished by its unique properties. When sprayed, it expands rapidly, filling every nook and cranny, conforming perfectly to complex shapes and creating an airtight and moisture-resistant seal. This dual action is what sets spray foam apart from traditional insulation materials.

Types of Spray Foam Insulation

We primarily utilize two main types of spray foam insulation:

Closed-Cell Spray Foam: This type expands to create a dense, cellular structure with a high concentration of closed cells. It offers excellent structural strength, making it ideal for areas requiring added support, such as crawl spaces, attic floors, or below-grade applications. Its high R-value per square foot provides superior thermal resistance, significantly reducing heating and cooling costs. Closed-cell foam is also highly effective at blocking air and moisture infiltration, making it an excellent choice for controlling condensation and preventing issues like mold and mildew.

Open-Cell Spray Foam: This type expands to a lower density, creating a more flexible foam with a higher concentration of open cells. While it has a slightly lower R-value per inch than closed-cell foam, it still provides excellent insulation performance and is known for its sound-dampening properties. Open-cell foam adheres well to a variety of surfaces and is often used in interior applications like walls, ceilings, and attics where structural strength is not the primary concern, but air sealing and noise reduction are key benefits.
