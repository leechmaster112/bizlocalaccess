Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is paint protection film and how does it protect my car?

Paint protection film is a durable, transparent urethane layer applied to the painted surfaces of your vehicle. It acts as an invisible shield, absorbing impacts from road debris, stone chips, and minor scratches. The self-healing properties of our premium films allow minor swirl marks to disappear with heat, keeping your car's finish looking new and protecting its resale value.
