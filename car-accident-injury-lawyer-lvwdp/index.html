Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How soon do I need to contact a car accident lawyer in New York after my accident?

It's crucial to contact a lawyer promptly after your accident in New York. There are strict statutes of limitations, typically three years from the date of the accident, within which you must file a personal injury lawsuit. Early contact allows us to investigate thoroughly, gather evidence while it's often still available, and protect your legal rights.

Q: What should I do immediately after a car accident in New York?

First, ensure safety. Move to a secure location if possible without leaving the scene. Check for injuries and call emergency services if needed. Exchange information with the other driver(s) (name, contact details, insurance info, license plate numbers). Report the accident to the police and gather witness information. Avoid admitting fault or making recorded statements to insurance companies without consulting a lawyer.

Q: Will the insurance company pay for my injuries if I hire a lawyer?

Insurance companies aim to minimize payouts. Hiring a lawyer significantly increases your chances of receiving fair compensation. Our firm handles all communications and negotiations with your insurance company, leveraging our experience to advocate for your rights and ensure your legitimate claims are addressed properly.

Q: Do I have a case if I was partially at fault for the car accident?

In New York, comparative negligence applies. You can still recover damages even if you were partially at fault, but your compensation will be reduced by your percentage of fault. Our lawyers will carefully analyze the circumstances to determine liability and advise you on the strength of your claim.

Q: How long does a typical car accident injury case take in New York?

The duration varies significantly based on factors like injury severity, insurance cooperation, and the complexity of determining fault. Simple cases with clear liability and quick settlements might resolve in a few months. More complex cases involving severe injuries or disputes could take a year or longer. We will keep you informed throughout the process.
