Camden Enterprises

Camden Enterprises has proudly served Maryland’s Eastern Shore since 2004, helping homeowners create safer, more comfortable, and more functional spaces through expert remodeling and home improvement services. Specializing in custom walk-in showers, tub-to-shower conversions, bathroom remodeling, and kitchen renovations, our team focuses on solutions that improve everyday living while adding long-term value to the home. We’re especially passionate about helping seniors and aging homeowners maintain independence through accessible bathroom upgrades. Our walk-in shower installations and shower wall surround systems are designed to improve safety, reduce fall risks, and make daily routines easier without sacrificing style or comfort. Based in Princess Anne, MD, Camden Enterprises serves Salisbury and surrounding communities across Somerset, Wicomico, Worcester, and Accomack counties with quality craftsmanship, honest communication, and personalized service. Whether it’s a complete bathroom transformation, custom shower build, kitchen remodel, or seasonal snow removal, our goal is simple: deliver dependable work that makes life easier for our customers.
