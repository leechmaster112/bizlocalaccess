Solsure Electrical Ltd

Solsure Electrical Ltd is a Nottingham-based solar energy company and NICEIC-certified electrician serving residential and commercial clients across Nottingham, Nottinghamshire, and the wider East Midlands. From first-time solar adopters in Nottingham looking to reduce energy bills, to commercial properties across Nottinghamshire seeking electric vehicle charging infrastructure, every project is completed to the highest industry standards with full MCS certification - ensuring eligibility for government energy schemes and Smart Export Guarantee (SEG) payments. Free site surveys and no-obligation quotes are available for solar installations and EV charging projects throughout the region.
