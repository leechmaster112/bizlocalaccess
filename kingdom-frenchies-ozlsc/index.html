Location

Kingdom Frenchies operates with a profound commitment to responsible breeding practices. We understand that bringing a French Bulldog into your home is a significant and exciting decision, and we strive to ensure that every puppy we place comes from a home where they were cherished and given the best start in life. Our primary goal is not just to produce beautiful puppies, but to be a source of healthy, structurally sound companions with stable temperaments, ready to bring joy and laughter to their forever homes.

Our breeding program is built on a foundation of careful selection, health testing, and a deep passion for the French Bulldog breed. Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: Do you only ship puppies to Millersburg, OH?

A: Yes, due to our commitment to responsible breeding and ensuring the best possible care for our puppies, Kingdom Frenchies exclusively offers French Bulldog puppies for adoption to families residing locally in Millersburg, OH 44654. We prioritize the health and well-being of our puppies and believe that local placement allows for easier, more direct contact and support.

Q2: How long have you been breeding French Bulldogs?

A: Kingdom Frenchies has been dedicated to responsible French Bulldog breeding for several years. Our journey began with a shared passion for the breed and a commitment to improving health and temperament standards through careful selection and ethical practices.

Q3: What health clearances do you require for your breeding dogs?

A: Absolutely! The health and longevity of our puppies are paramount. Our breeding dogs undergo rigorous health testing, including clearances for common French Bulldog concerns such as Brachycephalic Airway Syndrome (BAS), Heart disease (like Patent Ductus Arteriosus - PDA and Mitral Valve Disease - MVD), Hip Dysplasia (as recommended by the Orthopedic Foundation for Animals - OFA), and others relevant to the breed. We provide documentation of these clearances to potential puppy owners.

Q4: What is your policy on puppy contracts and guarantees?

A: We are confident in the health and quality of our puppies and stand behind them. We provide a detailed contract outlining our policies, including a health guarantee that covers specific congenital issues for a defined period after the puppy's arrival. This guarantee reflects our commitment to responsible breeding and our desire for our puppies to thrive in their new homes.

Q5: Can I visit the puppies before bringing one home?

A: Yes, we encourage potential puppy owners to visit our facility in Millersburg. We believe seeing the puppies in their clean and safe environment is an important part of the decision-making process. Please contact us at +13304734057 or visit our website https://kingdomfrenchies.com/ to schedule a visit and meet the puppies.
