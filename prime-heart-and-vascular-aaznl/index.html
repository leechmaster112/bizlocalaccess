Our Services at 6201 Dallas Parkway, Suite 210

Our practice offers a comprehensive range of cardiology and vascular services designed to address various heart and blood vessel conditions. We pride ourselves on utilizing advanced technology and evidence-based medicine to deliver effective, patient-centered care.

Cardiology Services:

Our cardiology services focus on the heart and its associated structures. We diagnose and manage conditions such as coronary artery disease, heart rhythm disorders (arrhythmias), heart failure, heart valve problems, and high blood pressure (hypertension). We also provide preventive cardiology services to help you maintain heart health and reduce your risk of developing cardiovascular diseases.

Common diagnostic procedures performed at our Plano location include thorough physical examinations, electrocardiograms (EKGs), echocardiograms (ultrasound of the heart), stress testing, and lipid profile assessments. Based on the findings, our cardiologists may recommend lifestyle modifications, medications, or further interventions.

Vascular Services:

Our vascular services concentrate on the blood vessels outside of the heart – the arteries and veins that transport blood throughout the body. We diagnose and treat conditions affecting these vessels, such as peripheral artery disease (PAD), carotid artery disease, venous insufficiency, varicose veins, and deep vein thrombosis (DVT).

At our Plano facility, we utilize minimally invasive techniques whenever possible to diagnose and treat vascular conditions. These may include vascular ultrasounds, ankle-brachial index (ABI) tests, and procedures like angioplasty and stenting for narrowed arteries, or sclerotherapy and laser treatments for problematic veins.
