4. Why is regular sealcoating important for my paved surface?

Regular sealcoating is a crucial maintenance service that protects your pavement from damage caused by sun, rain, oil, and gasoline. It helps to prevent cracks and fading, significantly extending the lifespan of your pavement and keeping it looking new.

5. How do I know if my driveway or parking lot needs repairs or a full resurface?

Signs you may need repairs include visible cracks, potholes, or drainage issues. If the surface is extensively faded, brittle, and has widespread cracking, a full resurface might be the most cost-effective solution.
