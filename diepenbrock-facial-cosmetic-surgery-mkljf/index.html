Comprehensive Facial Cosmetic Services

Diepenbrock Facial Cosmetic Surgery offers a wide range of services designed to address various signs of aging and enhance facial features. Our Fort Wayne location provides access to both non-surgical and surgical options, allowing us to cater to different patient needs and preferences.

Non-Surgical Options:

For patients seeking effective results with minimal downtime, our Fort Wayne practice offers advanced non-surgical treatments:

Botox® Cosmetic: A highly effective treatment for reducing the appearance of dynamic wrinkles, such as crow's feet and forehead lines, by temporarily relaxing targeted facial muscles. Dermal Fillers: Using premium dermal fillers, we can restore lost volume, plump lips, enhance facial contours, and smooth static wrinkles, providing immediate and natural-looking volume restoration. Laser Skin Resurfacing: Utilizing state-of-the-art laser technology, we can effectively address concerns like sun damage, fine lines, wrinkles, skin texture, and pigment issues, promoting a more youthful and radiant complexion. Thread Lifts: These minimally invasive procedures can provide a subtle lift to the face and neck, stimulating collagen production for improved skin texture and tone.

Surgical Procedures:

For those seeking more dramatic and long-lasting results, our Fort Wayne surgical suite offers expertly performed facial rejuvenation surgery:

Facelift (Rhytidectomy): A comprehensive surgical procedure designed to lift and tighten sagging facial skin, redefine facial contours, and restore a more youthful profile. Neck Lift: Addresses jowls and a lax neck line, often combined with liposuction or platysmal muscle repair for a comprehensive rejuvenation of the lower face and neck. Brow Lift: Lifts the eyebrows and upper eyelids for a more alert and refreshed appearance, smoothing horizontal forehead lines and correcting hooding. Facelift with Facelift Implants: Offers enhanced support and structure for the midface, providing natural-looking volume restoration and improved definition.
