1. Botox Injections

Botox is a highly effective treatment for temporarily reducing the appearance of dynamic wrinkles caused by muscle movement. Injected into specific facial muscles, Botox works by blocking nerve signals that cause muscle contractions. This results in smoother skin and a more relaxed appearance. Ideal for treating areas like the forehead, between the eyebrows, and around the eyes (crow's feet).

2. Dermal Fillers

Dermal fillers are substances, most commonly based on hyaluronic acid, that are injected under the skin to restore lost volume, fill wrinkles, and enhance facial contours. They can be used to plump lips, smooth nasolabial folds, add volume to the cheeks and temples, and even provide subtle support for the jawline. Different types of fillers offer varying degrees of correction and longevity.
