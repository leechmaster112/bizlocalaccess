Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Gold IRA?

A Gold IRA is a type of Individual Retirement Arrangement that allows you to hold physical precious metals, such as gold and silver coins or bars, instead of or in addition to traditional financial investments like stocks and bonds. It is a self-directed IRA, giving you more control over your retirement assets.

How does funding a Gold IRA work?

Funding a Gold IRA typically involves setting up an account with a specialized custodian, then funding it through various means such as a direct rollover from an existing 401(k), IRA, or other eligible retirement plan, or by making new contributions if you meet the IRS guidelines for IRA contributions.

What are the rules for adding gold to my IRA?

The IRS has specific rules regarding precious metals IRAs. Generally, the metals must be held in an approved depository, meet certain purity standards (e.g., .995 fine gold), and be in the form of specific approved coins or bars. We guide you through these regulations to ensure compliance.

Is investing in gold through an IRA tax-advantaged?

Yes, like traditional IRAs, a Gold IRA offers tax-deferred growth (for traditional accounts) or tax-free growth (for Roth accounts) on your investments. However, withdrawals may be subject to taxes depending on the type of IRA and your age.

How is the gold stored in a Gold IRA?

Gold held within a compliant Precious Metals IRA must be stored in an IRS-approved depository. These are secure, insured facilities specifically designed for holding physical precious metals on behalf of IRA owners, ensuring the assets remain segregated and meet IRS requirements.
