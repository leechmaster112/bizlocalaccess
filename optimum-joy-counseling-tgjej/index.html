Frequently Asked Questions

What specific services do you offer?

At Optimum Joy Counseling, we primarily focus on providing expert care for individuals experiencing anxiety (including generalized anxiety, social anxiety, panic disorder, etc.) and those impacted by trauma (such as PTSD). Our services include individual therapy sessions utilizing evidence-based approaches like Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT), and Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing (EMDR). We also offer support for stress management, coping skills development, and overall emotional well-being. Please visit our website or call (312) 772-9796 for a comprehensive list of therapeutic modalities.

How do I get started with Optimum Joy Counseling?

Getting started is simple and convenient. You can initiate the process by calling our office directly at (312) 772-9796. Our friendly team will assist you in scheduling your first appointment, answer any preliminary questions you may have, and provide information about our therapists. Alternatively, you can explore our website, https://optimumjoy.com/, to learn more about our practice and potentially book an initial consultation online. During your first session, you'll have the opportunity to meet your therapist and discuss your needs and goals.

Are your therapists licensed?

Absolutely. The therapists at Optimum Joy Counseling are fully licensed mental health professionals in their respective states. They hold credentials such as Licensed Clinical Social Worker (LCSW), Licensed Professional Counselor (LPC), or Licensed Clinical Psychologist (PhD/LPC). This ensures you receive care from qualified and experienced practitioners dedicated to adhering to the highest ethical standards. You can inquire about specific therapist credentials during your initial contact or consultation.

How does a typical therapy session work?

A typical therapy session at Optimum Joy Counseling lasts approximately 50 minutes. Your first session usually involves an intake process where your therapist will gather information about your background, reasons for seeking therapy, and your treatment goals. Subsequent sessions involve collaborative discussion, guided exercises, and therapeutic techniques tailored to your needs. We strive to create a comfortable and confidential space for open dialogue. The frequency of sessions is typically discussed during your initial appointment and may vary based on individual needs and progress.

What is the cost of therapy, and do you accept insurance?

Therapy fees at Optimum Joy Counseling are based on factors like the therapist's experience and credentials, session length, and location. We strive to offer competitive rates and may provide information on potential sliding scale options or payment plans. Regarding insurance, we are currently an out-of-network provider for most major insurance plans. This means we do not directly bill insurance companies. We will provide you with a superbill after each session that you can submit to your insurance provider for potential reimbursement. We recommend contacting your insurance company directly to understand your out-of-network benefits before your first session.
