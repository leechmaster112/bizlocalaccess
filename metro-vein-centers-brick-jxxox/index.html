Location

Metro Vein Centers Brick, located at 525 Jack Martin Boulevard, Suite 200, Brick, NJ 08724, is a nationally accredited vein treatment center dedicated exclusively to serving residents of Brick and surrounding areas within a reasonable driving distance. Our Brick location offers state-of-the-art vein care utilizing the latest diagnostic technologies and evidence-based treatment protocols specifically tailored to address the unique vascular health needs of our local community.

At our Brick facility, we provide comprehensive venous diagnostics and minimally invasive treatment options for a wide range of venous conditions. Our team of vascular specialists, led by experienced phlebologists, focuses on delivering personalized care for patients suffering from spider veins, varicose veins, chronic venous insufficiency, and other venous disorders. We utilize advanced imaging techniques like duplex ultrasound to precisely diagnose vascular issues and develop targeted treatment plans.

Our innovative treatment approach at the Brick center includes cutting-edge procedures such as endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), radiofrequency ablation (RFA), sclerotherapy, and Varithena microfoam sclerotherapy. These minimally invasive techniques offer significant advantages over traditional surgical approaches, including smaller incisions, reduced scarring, faster recovery times, and minimal downtime. Most patients can return to their normal activities within one to two days following treatment.

We understand that vascular concerns can impact both physical comfort and quality of life. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What specific services do you offer at your Brick location?

We provide comprehensive vein treatment services including diagnostic ultrasound, sclerotherapy for spider veins, endovenous laser treatment (EVLT) for larger varicose veins, radiofrequency ablation (RFA), and Varithena microfoam sclerotherapy. Our Brick center focuses exclusively on these vein treatment services.

Q: How long does recovery take after vein treatment at your Brick facility?

Recovery time varies by treatment type. Most patients experience mild discomfort and can return to normal activities within 1-2 days after procedures like EVLT or RFA. Sclerotherapy typically requires minimal downtime, with most patients resuming daily activities immediately. We provide detailed post-treatment instructions during your consultation.

Q: Do you accept insurance for vein treatments in Brick?

We accept most major insurance plans and will verify your benefits during your initial consultation. Our Brick office staff will assist with insurance verification and help determine your coverage for specific procedures.

Q: What should I expect during my first visit to the Brick center?

Your initial visit includes a comprehensive vascular examination, duplex ultrasound imaging when needed, and a personalized treatment plan discussion. We begin with a thorough medical history review and physical examination, followed by any necessary diagnostic testing to accurately assess your venous condition.

Q: How often should I schedule follow-up care after treatment at your Brick location?

Follow-up appointments are typically scheduled 2-4 weeks after treatment to monitor healing and address any concerns. We may recommend periodic check-ups every 6-12 months to maintain optimal vein health, depending on your individual condition and treatment history.
