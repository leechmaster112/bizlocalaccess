Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

Q: Do I need prior experience to try axe throwing?

A: Absolutely not! We offer introductory sessions for complete beginners. Our experienced staff will guide you through the basics, ensuring you have a safe and fun time.

Q: How safe is axe throwing at your location?

A: Safety is our top priority. We use professional-grade equipment, enforce strict rules, and provide mandatory safety gear like helmets. Our trained staff supervise all sessions to ensure a secure environment.

Q: What is the minimum age for participants?

A: Children must be at least 10 years old to participate. Minors under 18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Q: Can I book a private event or team-building session?

A: Yes! We offer private events and team-building packages. Please contact us at (732) 555-4812 or visit our website to learn more about booking a custom experience.

Q: What should I wear to an axe throwing session?

A: We recommend wearing comfortable clothing and closed-toe shoes. Please bring a valid photo ID if you are under 18. Helmets and protective gear will be provided.
