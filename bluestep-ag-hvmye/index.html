Bluestep AG is a Swiss consultancy based in Zürich, specialising in executive search, interim management, strategic consulting, and innovation services exclusively for the healthcare sector. With a tailored, step-by-step approach, the firm supports hospitals, clinics, and healthcare organisations in leadership recruitment, succession planning, quality management (including ISO and EFQM systems), and the development of new healthcare initiatives. Bluestep combines discretion, expertise, and practical tools to deliver sustainable, effective solutions that align with the unique demands of the healthcare environment.
