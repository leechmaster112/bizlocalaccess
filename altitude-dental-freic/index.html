Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What services does Altitude Dental offer?

We provide a comprehensive range of dental services, including preventive care (cleanings, check-ups), restorative treatments (fillings, crowns, root canals), and cosmetic dentistry (teeth whitening, veneers).

Q2: How often should I visit the dentist?

We recommend visiting the dentist for a check-up and cleaning every six months. However, the frequency may vary depending on your individual oral health needs.

Q3: Does Altitude Dental offer payment plans?

Yes, we understand that dental care can be a significant investment, and we offer various payment options to help make it more manageable for our patients.

Q4: What is the process for getting dental implants?

Getting dental implants involves several steps, including an initial consultation, X-rays or scans, surgical placement of the implant, and a healing period followed by the attachment of the final restoration. Our team will guide you through each step.
