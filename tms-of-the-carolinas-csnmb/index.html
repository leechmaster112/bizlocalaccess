What is TMS Therapy?

Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) therapy is a non-surgical procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate specific areas of the brain. It is particularly effective for individuals who have not found sufficient relief from traditional antidepressant medications. The treatment involves a device that delivers focused magnetic pulses to the scalp, which then activate nerve cells in the brain that are involved in mood regulation. Unlike electroconvulsive therapy (ECT), TMS is non-invasive and does not require anesthesia or sedation, allowing patients to resume their normal activities immediately after each session.

The TMS Treatment Process

The TMS treatment process at our Fayetteville location is designed to be straightforward and manageable. A typical course of treatment involves daily sessions, five days a week, for a period of four to six weeks. Each session lasts approximately 20 to 40 minutes. During the procedure, you will be seated comfortably, and a specialized device will be placed on your head. You will feel a tapping sensation as the magnetic pulses are delivered. You can read, listen to music, or simply relax during your treatment. Our trained technicians will monitor your progress closely throughout the entire course of therapy.
