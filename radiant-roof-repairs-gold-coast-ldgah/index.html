Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What roofing services do you offer?

We provide a comprehensive range of services including roof inspections, leak repairs, storm damage assessment and repair, full roof replacement using various materials (asphalt, metal, slate, tile), and general roof maintenance.

How long does a typical roof repair or replacement take?

The duration depends on the scope of the work, the size of the roof, and weather conditions. Minor repairs can often be completed the same day or within a few days. A full replacement may take a few days to a week. We provide you with a clear timeline once your specific project is assessed.

Do you handle insurance claims for storm damage?

Yes, we understand that storm damage can be stressful and often involves insurance claims. We can assist you in coordinating with your insurance provider, guiding you through the claims process, and liaising with your insurer to ensure your claim is handled efficiently.

What types of roofing materials do you use?

We work with a variety of high-quality materials suitable for different climates and budgets, including durable asphalt shingles, long-lasting metal roofing (steel, copper, aluminium), traditional slate and clay tiles, and more. We can advise on the best material for your specific situation.

Are your services available for both residential and commercial properties?

Absolutely. Whether you're a homeowner in Mermaid Waters or a business owner in New York, our team has the expertise to handle roofing needs of all types and scales.
