Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What exactly is kitchen cabinet respray?

A: Kitchen cabinet respray is the process of stripping old paint or veneer from your existing cabinets and applying a fresh coat of paint using specialised spraying techniques. This gives your cabinets a brand-new look without the need for replacement.

Q2: How long does a typical kitchen cabinet respray project take?

A: The duration depends on the size of your kitchen and the number of cabinets being resprayed. Generally, you can expect the process to take anywhere from 2 to 5 days, including preparation, spraying, and the final protective coating application. We will provide a precise timeline during your consultation at our Flat 7 Passenger House, William Morris Way location.

Q3: What types of cabinet materials can you respray?

A: We primarily specialise in respraying melamine, MDF, and laminated kitchen cabinets. Our expertise ensures that we achieve excellent results on these common materials, providing a durable and attractive finish.

Q4: Do you handle the preparation of the cabinets myself, or is that included?

A: Preparation is a crucial part of achieving a lasting finish and is included in our service. Our team at Flat 7 Passenger House, William Morris Way will handle the complete preparation process, including cleaning, sanding, and priming, before applying the new paint.

Q5: What kind of guarantee do you offer on your respray work?

A: We stand behind the quality of our workmanship and provide a guarantee on the paint finish. The specifics of the guarantee, including duration and terms, will be discussed and agreed upon during your project consultation at our studio in London, SW6 2UP.
