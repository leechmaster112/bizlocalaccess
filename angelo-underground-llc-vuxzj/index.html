Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need permits for excavation work?

Often, yes. Many excavation projects, especially those involving public roads or significant site disturbance, require permits from the City of Portland or Multnomah County. Angelo Underground LLC can assist you in determining permit requirements and navigating the application process to ensure your project complies with all local regulations.

How long does a typical excavation or trenching project take?

The timeline varies significantly based on the project's scope, complexity, site conditions, and weather. Simple trenching for a short run might take just a few hours, while larger excavation projects like foundation work can span several days or weeks. We provide realistic timelines during our initial consultation after assessing your specific needs.

Do you handle debris removal after excavation?

Absolutely. As part of our comprehensive excavation services, we manage the removal of excess soil and debris from your site. We coordinate with appropriate disposal facilities to ensure waste is handled responsibly and efficiently, leaving your property clean and ready for the next phase.

Are your operators certified and experienced?

Yes. Our team consists of certified and highly experienced operators who are trained in the latest industry practices and equipment operation. Safety is paramount, and our crew members are proficient in identifying potential hazards and adhering to strict safety protocols on the job site.

What should I do if I have a problem with my existing underground utilities?

If you suspect an issue with underground utilities (like gas, water, or electrical lines), DO NOT attempt to dig yourself. This can be extremely dangerous. Contact your local utility company immediately to locate their lines using their services. Once confirmed safe, you can then contact Angelo Underground LLC for excavation work. We always call before digging to prevent accidental damage.
