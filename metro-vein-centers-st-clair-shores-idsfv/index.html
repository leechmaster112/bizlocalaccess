Our Services

At our St. Clair Shores location, we offer a comprehensive range of vein treatments tailored to address both cosmetic and medical concerns:

Varicose Vein Treatment: We utilize cutting-edge techniques like EVLT (Endovenous Laser Treatment) and VNUS (Radiofrequency Ablation) to safely eliminate enlarged veins while preserving healthy circulation.

Spider & Telangiectasia Vein Therapy: Our sclerotherapy and Microphlebectomy procedures target delicate, surface-level veins for smoother, more youthful-looking skin.

Chronic Venous Insufficiency Management: We diagnose and treat underlying venous reflux disease through targeted procedures that alleviate symptoms like swelling, aching, and skin changes.

Preventive Care: Our thorough vascular evaluations help identify risk factors and implement personalized prevention strategies for optimal long-term results.
