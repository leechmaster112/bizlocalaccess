Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care - Pearland

Welcome to Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care in Pearland, your dedicated partner for comprehensive pet wellness and urgent care. Located at 11150 Broadway St Suite 120, we provide expert veterinary services tailored to the unique needs of pets residing in this community. Our team of compassionate, board-certified veterinarians and skilled technicians is committed to delivering the highest standard of care in a state-of-the-art facility.

Comprehensive Veterinary Services

At our Pearland location, we offer a full spectrum of veterinary care designed to keep your beloved companions healthy and happy. Our preventive care services include thorough physical examinations, tailored vaccination protocols, and essential parasite control programs. We emphasize the importance of regular check-ups as the foundation of pet wellness, helping you establish a proactive healthcare plan specific to your pet's age, breed, and lifestyle.

Our hospital features advanced diagnostic capabilities, including in-house laboratory testing and digital radiography, allowing us to quickly and accurately diagnose health concerns. We provide comprehensive dental care, from routine cleanings to dental surgery, recognizing that oral health is integral to overall wellbeing. Our experienced team performs a wide range of surgical procedures, from routine spay and neuter operations to more complex orthopedic and soft tissue surgeries.

Urgent Care & Emergency Services

Recognizing that pet emergencies can happen anytime, Petfolk offers prompt, professional urgent care services. Our facility is equipped to handle a variety of acute conditions, from trauma and poisoning incidents to gastrointestinal obstructions and heatstroke. While we may not provide 24/7 emergency services, our team is prepared to address time-sensitive medical issues that require immediate attention but are not life-threatening emergencies.

We understand that finding the right veterinary care can be stressful for pet owners. That's why we've created a comfortable, stress-free environment where pets receive gentle handling and owners receive clear, compassionate communication. Our focus is on building lasting relationships with both pets and their owners, providing not just treatment but ongoing support for your furry family members.

Expert Team & Modern Facility

Whether your pet requires routine wellness care, needs emergency treatment, or requires specialized medical attention, our team at Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care is here to provide expert care with compassion.
