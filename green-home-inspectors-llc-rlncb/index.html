Our Home Inspection Services

Our standard home inspection covers a wide range of components crucial to a property's functionality and safety. Our inspection includes, but is not limited to:

• Foundation and Structure: Assessment of the foundation, walls, floors, and roof structure for signs of damage, movement, or instability.

• Electrical Systems: Evaluation of the electrical panel, wiring, outlets, and circuit breakers for code compliance and safety hazards.

• Plumbing Systems: Examination of accessible plumbing fixtures, water heaters, drainage systems, and water pressure.

• HVAC Systems: Review of heating, ventilation, and air conditioning systems for efficiency, functionality, and age.

• Interior Components: Inspection of ceilings, walls, doors, windows, and built-in cabinetry.

• Tankless Water Heaters: Assessment of the functionality and safety of tankless water heater installations.

• Pest Issues: Visual inspection for signs of common pests like termites and rodents.

• Attic, Basement, and Crawl Spaces: Evaluation of these areas for accessibility, insulation, ventilation, and potential moisture issues.

Our inspection process is meticulous and client-focused. We begin with a detailed walk-through of the property, explaining our findings as we go. Following the visual inspection, our inspector compiles a comprehensive report outlining the condition of each major system and component. This report is clear, concise, and includes detailed descriptions, photographs, and explanations of any identified issues.
