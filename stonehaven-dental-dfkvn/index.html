Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

Question: Do you accept dental insurance?

Answer: Yes, Stonehaven Dental works with most major dental insurance plans. Please contact our office to discuss your specific benefits and coverage.

Question: What forms of payment do you accept?

Answer: We accept cash, checks, credit cards, and debit cards. We also accept most major dental insurance plans and offer financing options for qualified patients.

Question: How often should I schedule dental check-ups?

Answer: We generally recommend bi-annual dental check-ups and cleanings for most patients. However, the frequency may vary based on individual needs, which our team will determine during your examination.

Question: Do you offer emergency dental appointments?

Answer: Yes, we understand dental emergencies can happen anytime. Please call our office, and we will do our best to accommodate you promptly.

Question: What is the process for getting dental implants?

Answer: The process typically involves several stages, including initial consultation, implant placement, a healing period, and finally, the placement of the permanent restoration. Our team will guide you through each step with detailed information and support.
