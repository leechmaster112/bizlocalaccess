Q: What is the process for getting a dental filling done?

Getting a dental filling typically involves a few steps: first, we will examine your tooth, possibly take X-rays, and then remove the decayed portion. After preparing the area, we will place the filling material and shape it to match your natural tooth. Finally, we will polish the filling to ensure a comfortable bite and a natural appearance.
