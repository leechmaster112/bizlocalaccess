What is Speech and Language Therapy?

Speech and language therapy addresses difficulties with:

Speech Sound Development: Struggles with producing clear, understandable speech sounds. Language Development: Challenges with understanding spoken or written language, or expressing thoughts and ideas verbally or in writing. Stuttering: Repetitive movements or sounds with speech. Fluency: Working on smooth, effortless speech delivery. Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC): Supporting communication for children who find verbal speech challenging.

Our Assessment and Therapy Process

At Articulate Kids, we begin with a thorough assessment to understand your child's unique needs. This involves gathering information about your child's developmental history, observing communication skills, and using standardized tests. Based on the assessment findings, our therapists develop a personalized therapy plan focused on goals relevant to your child and family.

Therapy sessions are tailored to the child's age, interests, and specific difficulties. We employ a range of engaging techniques designed to build skills in a fun and motivating way. Our approach focuses on practical communication skills that can be generalized to everyday situations.
