Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What conditions qualify for medical cannabis in Luzerne County?

A: Qualifying conditions can vary, but commonly include chronic pain, epilepsy, multiple sclerosis, chemotherapy side effects, PTSD, and other debilitating conditions determined by the state's medical cannabis program. We recommend consulting with your healthcare provider and reviewing Pennsylvania's specific qualifying conditions.

Q2: How do I register to purchase medical cannabis at Ethos Wilkes-Barre?

A: To purchase medical cannabis at our Wilkes-Barre location, you must first be certified by a Pennsylvania-licensed healthcare provider. This involves obtaining a medical marijuana certification form, which you can then submit to the Pennsylvania Medical Marijuana Program (PAMMP). Once approved, you will receive a Medical Marijuana Card, which allows you to register with a dispensary like Ethos Wilkes-Barre.

Q3: What forms of payment do you accept for medical cannabis purchases?

A: We accept payment via debit or credit cards, cash, and EBT (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program) benefits for approved patients. Please bring your valid Medical Marijuana Card and government-issued ID when making a purchase.

Q4: Are there any restrictions on the types of products I can purchase at Ethos Wilkes-Barre?

A: Yes, the products available for purchase are strictly regulated by the Pennsylvania Department of Health. We only sell items that are approved for medical use under the state's medical marijuana program. This includes flower, edibles, topicals, concentrates, and other ancillary products. All products are clearly labeled with relevant information.
