Q: Do I have a valid claim if I was partially at fault for the Port St. Lucie car accident?

In Florida, which follows a modified comparative negligence rule, you may still be able to recover damages even if you were partially at fault. However, your recovery may be reduced by your percentage of fault. A Port St. Lucie lawyer can evaluate the specifics of your accident to determine the viability of your claim.
