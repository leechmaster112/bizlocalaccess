Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of eye diseases do you treat at your hospital?

We treat a wide range of eye conditions including cataracts, diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma, corneal issues, refractive errors (requiring glasses or laser surgery), and provide comprehensive eye examinations for all ages.

Q2: Do you offer laser eye surgery?

Yes, our facility is equipped to perform various types of laser eye surgery, such as LASIK and LASEK, to correct common refractive errors like myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism.

Q3: Can I get an appointment for a routine eye check-up?

Yes, we offer routine eye check-ups. You can book an appointment by calling our phone number, 082142 63333.

Q4: What is the procedure for getting a cataract surgery?

The process involves a comprehensive eye examination, consultation with our ophthalmologist to determine suitability, discussing the procedure details, and scheduling the surgery at our hospital.

Q5: Are emergency eye services available?

While we primarily focus on scheduled appointments, our experienced team is available to assess and provide urgent care for sudden vision changes or other eye emergencies during regular business hours.
