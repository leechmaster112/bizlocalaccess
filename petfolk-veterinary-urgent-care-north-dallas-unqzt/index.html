Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care - North Dallas

Located at 17062 Preston Rd Suite 104, Dallas, TX 75248, Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care stands as a premier destination for pet wellness and emergency needs in the heart of North Dallas. Founded and led by a team of dedicated, expert veterinarians, this clinic has established itself as a trusted provider of comprehensive animal healthcare services for discerning pet owners seeking top-tier care for their beloved companions.

Our mission is to deliver exceptional veterinary medicine that prioritizes both the health and comfort of your pets. We understand that pets are integral members of the family, and we approach each case with the compassion and expertise required to address a wide spectrum of needs. From routine wellness examinations to unexpected emergencies, our state-of-the-art facility is designed to provide immediate, effective solutions tailored to your pet's specific requirements.

Our Services Include:

Comprehensive Wellness Care: Regular check-ups, vaccinations, parasite prevention, and dental care form the foundation of our approach to pet health. We emphasize preventative medicine to help keep your pet healthy and avoid potential issues before they become serious.

Advanced Diagnostic Capabilities: Our clinic is equipped with modern diagnostic tools, including digital radiography and in-house laboratory testing, allowing us to quickly and accurately assess your pet's condition.

Urgent Care & Emergency Services: We offer prompt attention for acute illnesses, injuries, and other time-sensitive conditions that require immediate veterinary intervention but may not necessitate a full emergency room visit.

Surgical Procedures: Our facility performs a range of soft tissue and orthopedic surgeries under the guidance of experienced veterinarians, utilizing advanced techniques and pain management protocols.

Behavioral Consultation: We provide expert guidance on pet behavior issues, helping owners establish positive relationships and address concerns ranging from anxiety to training difficulties.

At Petfolk, we pride ourselves on creating a stress-free environment for pets and their owners. Our team of compassionate professionals is committed to building lasting relationships with clients, ensuring that every visit is as comfortable and informative as possible.

For comprehensive pet healthcare needs, including routine check-ups and urgent veterinary attention, Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care at 17062 Preston Rd Suite 104 is your dedicated partner in pet wellness. We are fully equipped to handle a wide array of conditions, from minor concerns to complex medical issues, all within the walls of our North Dallas facility.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of pets do you treat?

We provide comprehensive care for dogs, cats, birds, rabbits, rodents, and other common household pets.

Q2: Do you offer emergency services?

Yes, our facility is equipped to handle after-hours emergencies. We provide urgent care for a variety of conditions requiring immediate attention.

Q3: What is your appointment process for routine care?

We recommend scheduling regular wellness examinations for optimal pet health. Walk-in availability is limited, so we encourage advance scheduling for non-urgent matters.

Q4: Do you accept pet insurance?

We accept most pet insurance plans and can assist with the claims process to help reduce your out-of-pocket expenses.

Q5: What should I bring to my pet's appointment?

Please bring your pet's medical records, a list of current medications, and any relevant vaccination history. We also recommend bringing a carrier or leash for your pet's comfort.
