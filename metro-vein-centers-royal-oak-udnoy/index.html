Location

Metro Vein Centers Royal Oak, located at 1103 N Main St Suite E, Royal Oak, MI 48067, is a nationally accredited vein treatment clinic dedicated exclusively to providing advanced care for individuals seeking relief from venous conditions. We understand that concerns about visible veins or discomfort can impact your daily life, and our state-of-the-art facility in Royal Oak is committed to offering the latest, innovative vein treatment technologies right here in your community.

Our focus at Metro Vein Centers Royal Oak is solely on diagnosing and treating venous diseases and conditions. Our team of experienced vascular specialists utilizes cutting-edge techniques to deliver effective, minimally invasive solutions tailored to each patient's specific needs. We pride ourselves on providing personalized care within our comfortable and modern clinic setting at 1103 N Main St Suite E.

Common issues addressed at our Royal Oak location include spider veins, which are small, dilated veins appearing close to the skin's surface, often causing cosmetic concerns. We also treat larger varicose veins, which are swollen, twisted veins that can cause pain, heaviness, swelling, and skin discoloration. Our treatments are designed to be effective, comfortable, and typically allow patients to return to their normal activities quickly.

At Metro Vein Centers Royal Oak, we utilize the most advanced technologies available in the field of phlebology (vein treatment). These include:

Laser Treatment (Nd:YAG): A highly effective method for treating spider veins and smaller varicose veins using focused light energy. Radiance™ Laser System: An advanced laser platform designed for optimal results in treating both spider and varicose veins with minimal discomfort. Venaseal™: A medical-grade adhesive used to close varicose veins during a minimally invasive procedure. Mechanical Chemical Ablation (MCA): A technique using a sclerosing solution injected into the vein to cause its closure. Microphlebectomy: A procedure to remove varicose veins through tiny incisions, resulting in excellent cosmetic outcomes.

Our commitment at Metro Vein Centers Royal Oak is to provide expert diagnosis and compassionate treatment for all venous concerns affecting patients living in the Royal Oak area. We believe in educating our patients about their conditions and the available treatment options, ensuring they feel confident and informed about their care journey right here at our facility.
