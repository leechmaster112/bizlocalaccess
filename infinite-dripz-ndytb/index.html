Frequently Asked Questions

What is IV therapy?

Intravenous (IV) therapy involves delivering fluids, vitamins, minerals, and other nutrients directly into your bloodstream through an IV drip. This method bypasses the digestive system, allowing for faster and more efficient absorption of nutrients compared to oral supplements.

How does IV therapy work?

During your visit, a trained healthcare professional will start an IV line using a small, sterile needle. The IV fluid is then administered slowly over a period, typically 30-90 minutes, depending on the specific treatment. You'll be comfortable, hydrated, and can relax during the process.

Are your IVs and vitamins safe?

Absolutely. Infinite Dripz uses only FDA-approved, high-quality ingredients and pharmaceutical-grade solutions. Our facility maintains strict sanitation protocols, and our staff follows comprehensive health and safety guidelines to ensure a safe and effective experience for every client.

What conditions can IV therapy help with?

IV therapy can be beneficial for various purposes, including boosting energy levels, enhancing athletic recovery, supporting detoxification, strengthening the immune system, alleviating symptoms of chronic fatigue, improving skin health, and providing general wellness support.

How often should I get IV therapy?

The frequency of IV treatments depends on your individual health goals and lifestyle. Some clients opt for regular sessions (e.g., weekly or bi-weekly) for consistent results, while others choose IV therapy as needed for specific situations, such as before a big event or when feeling particularly depleted. Our team can help you determine the optimal schedule based on your needs.
