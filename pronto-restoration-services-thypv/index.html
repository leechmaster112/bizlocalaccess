Pronto Restoration Services at our Frisco location handles all types of water damage, including but not limited to burst pipes, leaking roofs, appliance leaks, foundation issues, and flooding from external sources. Our expertise covers minor incidents and major disasters.

2. How quickly can you respond to an emergency?

Our process generally involves: emergency contact and assessment, water extraction using powerful pumps and vacuums, comprehensive drying using industrial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers, detailed cleaning and sanitizing, and finally, restoration of affected surfaces and structures.
