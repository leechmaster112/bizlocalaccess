Preschool Programs

Our preschool program prepares children for the transition to kindergarten and beyond. We focus on developing essential literacy and numeracy skills through play-based learning activities. Children engage in structured group activities as well as independent exploration, fostering both academic readiness and social competence.

Our preschool curriculum includes:

Literacy Development: Activities focused on phonics, reading readiness, vocabulary building, and comprehension skills. Mathematics: Concepts related to counting, sorting, patterns, shapes, measurement, and problem-solving. Science Exploration: Hands-on experiments and observations to cultivate natural curiosity. Arts and Creativity: Opportunities for self-expression through various art forms. Social Studies: Learning about community helpers, holidays, traditions, and the world around them.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What ages does Helping Hands Child Development Center serve?

We offer programs for infants, toddlers, preschoolers, and pre-kindergarten children. Our daycare services cater to children from approximately 6 months up to 5 years old.

2. What is the enrollment process?

To enroll your child, please contact our center using the provided phone number or visit our website. We will guide you through the application process, which typically includes an application form, background checks, enrollment agreement, and registration fees.

3. What is the daily schedule like?

Our schedule varies depending on the program but generally includes arrival, morning activities, lunch/snack, nap time (for younger children), afternoon activities, and dismissal. We offer flexible hours to accommodate different family schedules.

4. What is the curriculum focus?

Our curriculum is child-centered and focuses on developmental appropriateness. We emphasize literacy, mathematics, science, social studies, creative arts, and physical development through play-based learning and structured activities.

5. Are meals and snacks provided?

We provide nutritious morning and afternoon snacks. We also offer optional lunch programs where we provide a balanced lunch for children. Parents are welcome to send packed lunches if preferred.
