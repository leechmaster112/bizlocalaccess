Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What services do you offer at Paoli Nail Spa?

We provide a comprehensive range of nail services including manicures, pedicures, acrylic nail application, nail extensions, basic nail care, and specialized spa treatments designed to nourish and revitalize your hands and feet.

Q2: How long does a typical manicure or pedicure take?

A standard manicure usually takes about 30-45 minutes, while a pedicure can range from 45 minutes to an hour, depending on the specific treatment chosen and the condition of your feet.

Q3: Do you offer nail designs or colors?

Absolutely! We offer a wide selection of nail polish colors and can apply intricate designs to complement your style. Discuss your preferences with our team when you book your appointment.

Q4: Can I purchase nail polish or other products from you?

Yes, we carry a selection of high-quality nail care products and polishes. You can inquire about our current inventory and purchase items to take home with you.

Q5: Do you offer package deals or gift certificates for the Paoli location?

We do offer package deals for regular maintenance and gift certificates for clients visiting our Paoli Nail Spa location. Please ask our staff about our current promotions and options.
