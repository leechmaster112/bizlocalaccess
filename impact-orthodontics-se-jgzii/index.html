Frequently Asked Questions
Q1: What age is recommended for a child's first orthodontic visit at Impact Orthodontics SE?

A1: The American Association of Orthodontists recommends a child's first orthodontic visit by age seven. This allows us to monitor their growth and development and identify any potential issues early on, even if treatment isn't needed immediately. Our office at 280 Midpark Way SE Suite 208 is ready to welcome your child for this important evaluation.

Q2: What types of orthodontic treatments are available for teens at 280 Midpark Way SE Suite 208?

A2: At Impact Orthodontics SE, we offer a comprehensive range of orthodontic treatments for teens, including traditional braces and other advanced options designed to effectively correct alignment and bite issues. We tailor each treatment plan to the individual needs of your teen.

Q3: How long does orthodontic treatment typically last?

A3: The duration of orthodontic treatment varies depending on the complexity of the case and the individual's response to treatment. Most treatments at our 280 Midpark Way SE Suite 208 location can range from 12 to 24 months. We will provide a more precise estimate during your initial consultation.

Q4: Does Impact Orthodontics SE offer flexible payment options?

A4: Yes, we understand that orthodontic treatment is an investment. We offer various payment plans and work with many insurance providers to make treatment accessible. Please discuss your financial concerns with our team at 280 Midpark Way SE Suite 208, and we will do our best to accommodate you.

Q5: What should I expect during my child's initial orthodontic consultation at 280 Midpark Way SE Suite 208?

A5: During your initial consultation, our orthodontist will perform a thorough examination, review your child's medical and dental history, and discuss your smile goals. We will also take diagnostic records, such as X-rays and photos, to create a personalized treatment plan. This is also a great opportunity for you to ask any questions you may have about the process.
