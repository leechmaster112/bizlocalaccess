Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What types of custom t-shirts do you offer?

We offer a wide range of custom t-shirt options, including traditional screen printing, direct-to-garment (DTG) printing, heat transfer vinyl (HTV), and sublimation printing. We also specialize in printing on various shirt styles and fabrics, such as 100% cotton tees, poly-cotton blends, mesh shirts, performance shirts, and twill polos.

Do you help with design creation?

Yes! While we welcome your own design files (in formats like JPG, PNG, PDF, AI, or EPS), we also offer design assistance. If you're unsure about your design or need help bringing your idea to life, our team can provide suggestions, basic design layouts, or even help you create a simple graphic using our online design tools or during a consultation.

What is your minimum order quantity?

We strive to be flexible and accommodate a variety of needs. While bulk screen printing is our specialty and generally requires a minimum order (often 25-50 shirts, but this can vary), we also offer smaller quantity options through DTG printing or potentially smaller screen runs depending on the design complexity and fabric type. Please contact us at (513) 732-9690 or via our website for specific quotes based on your requirements.

How long does it typically take to get my custom t-shirts?

Our production time depends on the complexity of your order, the printing method chosen, the quantity, and our current workload. Simple screen printing jobs for bulk orders might take 5-10 business days, while DTG printing or smaller runs could be ready in 3-5 business days. Rush options are often available for an additional fee. We always provide you with a clear turnaround time estimate once your order is confirmed.

How do I place an order for custom t-shirts?

Ordering is easy! You can place an order online through our website https://www.freshrags.com/, where you can select your shirt style, fabric, quantity, printing method, and upload your design or request one. Alternatively, you can call us directly at (513) 732-9690 to discuss your project, ask questions, and get a quote. We can also provide a detailed quote based on your specific requirements.
