Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What services do you offer for pets at the Far North Central location?

We offer a comprehensive range of veterinary services including routine check-ups, vaccinations, parasite prevention and treatment, dental care, microchipping, wellness blood panels, minor surgical procedures, and internal medicine consultations.

Q2: Do you provide emergency veterinary care?

Yes, our clinic is equipped to handle urgent veterinary care needs. We offer prompt attention for accidents, sudden illness, trauma, and other unexpected health issues for your pets.

Q3: What are your business hours?

Our regular business hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Saturday 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, and Sunday 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM. Our urgent care services may have extended hours, please call for specific inquiries.

Q4: Can you perform dental procedures?

Absolutely. Our experienced team performs thorough dental cleanings, extractions, and oral surgeries using state-of-the-art equipment and techniques to ensure your pet's dental health is maintained.

Q5: Do you offer pet boarding or grooming services?

Currently, we focus solely on providing exceptional veterinary and urgent care services at our Far North Central location. We do not offer boarding or grooming services on-site.
