How soon should I see a doctor after a car accident in Columbia?

It is highly recommended to seek a medical evaluation as soon as possible after a car accident, even if you do not immediately feel pain. Many injuries, such as whiplash, have symptoms that can take hours or days to appear. Our team at Lordex Spine Center Of Columbia can provide a prompt assessment.
