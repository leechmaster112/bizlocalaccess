Fresh Pond Reservation Dog Park

Fresh Pond Reservation is a 162-acre shared-use off-leash area surrounding a 155-acre reservoir in Cambridge. A 2.2-mile loop trail passes wooded areas and meadows, and a designated dog beach lets Cambridge residents' dogs legally swim. Hours, photos, and directions are on its Doggie Park Near Me listing.

Frequently Asked Questions:

Q: Is the entire 162-acre Fresh Pond Reservation area off-leash for dogs?

A: Fresh Pond Reservation is a shared-use space, and specific designated areas are designated as off-leash for dogs. Please observe posted signage to ensure compliance and a positive experience for all visitors.

Q: What kind of terrain can I expect for my dog to run on?

A: You'll find a variety of natural terrain around the reservoir, including grassy areas and walking paths, offering diverse opportunities for your dog's enjoyment and exercise.

Q: Are there any specific rules I should be aware of before bringing my dog?

A: Yes, as a shared-use park, it's important to follow all posted rules and regulations, including leash requirements in non-off-leash zones and responsible pet ownership guidelines, to ensure everyone's safety and enjoyment.

Q: Can I bring my dog to the reservoir itself?

A: While the dog park is adjacent to the beautiful 155-acre reservoir, dogs must remain in designated off-leash areas and on-leash on trails surrounding the water, respecting conservation efforts and water quality.

Q: Is there parking available at the dog park entrance?

A: Parking is available at the 250 Fresh Pond Pkwy, Cambridge, MA 02138 location, providing convenient access to the dog park amenities.
