Balboa Fun Tours & Rentals

At Balboa Fun Tours in Newport Beach, we offer everything you need for a perfect beach day or water adventure. From beach gear to bikes and paddle crafts, we’ve got you covered whether you're exploring Newport Beach, Costa Mesa, Huntington Beach, Laguna Beach, Irvine, Seal Beach, or Long Beach. Here are our rental options: Bikes & Electric Bikes Beach Cruisers – Classic cruisers ideal for riding along the boardwalk or through Balboa Peninsula. Electric Bikes – A powered ride for going further with less effort; great for exploring the harbor, Newport Point, or coastal paths. Prices: Beach Cruisers ~ $20/day; Electric Bikes ~ $65/day. Multi-day and weekly rentals available. Kayaks & Paddle Boards (SUPs) Single Kayaks – Solo paddling in calm waters, perfect for getting up close to sea life or just enjoying the view across the bay. Double Kayaks – Bring a friend; share the paddle and the experience. Stand Up Paddle Boards (SUPs) – Enjoy a more active, standing-on-the-water view of the coastline. Beach Gear / Beach Stuff Surf Boards – Try your hand at catching a wave or simply ride the swell near the piers. Body Boards – For lighter waves, family fun, or beginner surf. Beach Chairs & Umbrellas – Relax in style with shade and comfort. Children’s Attachments & Tandem Bikes Baby Seats, Trailers, Trail-a-Bikes, Tandem Bikes – Great for families wanting to include kids in the ride safely and comfortably. Why Rent With Us Largest fleet in Orange County. Flexible rental durations: hours, full day, multi-day, weekly, even up to 30 days. Ideal location: 2000 W Balboa Blvd in Newport Beach, just a short drive from Costa Mesa, Huntington Beach, Laguna Beach, Irvine, Seal Beach, and Long Beach.
