Frequently Asked Questions

1. What specific services does Metro Vein Centers Brick offer?

Our Brick location specializes in comprehensive vein treatment services, including diagnostic ultrasound, endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), radiofrequency ablation (RFA), sclerotherapy, and other minimally invasive procedures for spider veins, varicose veins, and chronic venous conditions.

2. How long is the recovery period after vein treatment at your Brick facility?

Recovery varies by treatment type, but most patients experience minimal downtime. You can typically return to normal activities within a day or two after procedures like EVLT or RFA, with full recovery taking about 1-2 weeks. Sclerotherapy allows for immediate return to daily activities.

3. Do I need a referral to schedule an appointment at Metro Vein Centers Brick?

While referrals are not required, we welcome patients who schedule consultations directly. For complex cases, our physician may request additional diagnostic information before proceeding with treatment.

4. What conditions qualify for treatment at your Brick location?

We treat a range of conditions including symptomatic varicose veins, spider veins, reticulated veins, telangiectasias, chronic venous insufficiency, edema, and venous pain. Our physician determines eligibility based on individual health factors.

5. How soon can I schedule a vein consultation after contacting your Brick office?

We strive to accommodate new vein consultation requests within 1-2 weeks. You can expedite this process by contacting us directly at 732-782-9709.
