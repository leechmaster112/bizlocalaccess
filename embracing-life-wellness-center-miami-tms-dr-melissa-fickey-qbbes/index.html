FAQ

1. What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Coral Gables location?

TMS therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our clinic at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711, you will sit in a comfortable chair while a small electromagnetic coil is placed gently against your scalp. The treatment is administered in a private room and you are awake and alert throughout the approximately 20-minute session.

2. Do I need a referral to be seen at your Coral Gables office?

While a referral from your primary care physician or psychiatrist is helpful, it is not always required. We encourage you to contact our office directly at (305) 686-5150 to discuss your situation. Our team at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711 can guide you through the process of determining if TMS therapy is right for you.

3. How many treatment sessions will I need and how do I schedule them?

A typical TMS treatment course involves sessions five days a week for approximately 4-6 weeks, all conducted at our Coral Gables clinic. We understand this is a commitment, so we offer flexible scheduling to help you integrate treatment into your life. Our team will work with you to find appointment times that fit your schedule.

4. Will my insurance cover TMS therapy at your specific location?

TMS therapy is FDA-cleared and is covered by most major insurance providers for treatment-resistant depression. Our administrative team at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711 will verify your insurance benefits beforehand and handle all the paperwork, providing you with a clear understanding of any out-of-pocket costs prior to starting treatment.

5. Can I drive myself to and from my appointments at your Coral Gables clinic?

Yes, absolutely. One of the significant advantages of TMS therapy is that it does not require anesthesia or sedation. You can drive yourself to our office at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711, undergo your treatment, and drive yourself home or back to work immediately afterward without any downtime.
