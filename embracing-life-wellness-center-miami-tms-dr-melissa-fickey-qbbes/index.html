Our primary specialty at our Coral Gables clinic is Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) therapy. Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Coral Gables office?

TMS therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic pulses to stimulate the mood-regulating areas of the brain. At our clinic at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711, you will be seated in a comfortable chair. A magnetic coil is gently placed against your head, and you will hear a clicking sound and feel a light tapping sensation during the approximately 20-minute session. You are awake and alert throughout the entire process and can drive yourself home immediately afterward.

2. Do I need a referral to be seen at your Coral Gables location?

While a referral from your primary care physician or psychiatrist can be helpful, it is not a requirement to schedule a consultation at our office. You can contact us directly at (305) 686-5150 to book an appointment with Dr. Fickey to discuss if our services are the right fit for your needs.

3. How do I know if TMS therapy is right for me?

TMS therapy is primarily for individuals diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder who have not experienced adequate improvement from prior antidepressant medication. The best way to determine if you are a candidate is to schedule a comprehensive evaluation with Dr. Melissa Fickey at our Coral Gables clinic, where your specific history and symptoms can be reviewed in detail.

4. Are the services at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711 covered by insurance?

Many insurance plans do provide coverage for TMS therapy, especially after demonstrating that other treatments have been unsuccessful. Our administrative team at the Coral Gables office will work with you to verify your specific insurance benefits and outline any out-of-pocket costs before you begin treatment.

5. What makes Embracing Life Wellness Center unique in Coral Gables?

Our center is distinguished by its focused expertise in TMS therapy under the direct care of Dr. Melissa Fickey. We provide a highly personalized and integrative approach to mental health care, all within the comfortable and private setting of our Suite 711 clinic.
