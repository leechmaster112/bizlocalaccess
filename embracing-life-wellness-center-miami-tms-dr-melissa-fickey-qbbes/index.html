Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Coral Gables clinic?

TMS therapy is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our clinic at 2600 Douglas Rd Suite 711, you will be seated in a comfortable chair. A magnetic coil is placed gently against your scalp, delivering brief pulses. The procedure is performed while you are awake and alert, and typically lasts about 20-40 minutes.

2. Do I need a referral to be seen at Embracing Life Wellness Center in Coral Gables?

While a referral from your primary care physician or therapist can be helpful, it is not a requirement to schedule an appointment at our location. You can contact us directly at (305) 686-5150 to book a consultation with Dr. Fickey to discuss your needs.

3. What conditions do you treat at your 2600 Douglas Rd practice?

Our Coral Gables clinic specializes in the treatment of major depressive disorder and other mood-related conditions using TMS therapy. We also provide comprehensive psychiatric services, including evaluation, medication management, and psychotherapy for a range of mental health concerns.

4. How long does a typical course of TMS treatment take at your facility?

A typical TMS treatment course involves sessions five days a week for approximately 4-6 weeks. The total number of sessions is customized for each individual based on their specific response to the treatment, as determined by Dr. Fickey.

5. How can I schedule an appointment at your Coral Gables office?

Scheduling an appointment is simple. You can reach our office by calling (305) 686-5150. Our team will guide you through the process and answer any initial questions you may have. You can also learn more about our services by visiting our website at https://embracinglifetoday.com/.
