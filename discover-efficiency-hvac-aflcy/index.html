Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What types of HVAC systems do you install?

We specialize in installing a variety of efficient HVAC systems suitable for homes and businesses in Dartmouth, including high-efficiency furnaces, air conditioners, heat pumps, and ductless mini-split systems.

Q: How often should my HVAC system be serviced?

For optimal performance and longevity, we recommend professional HVAC maintenance at least once a year, typically before the start of the heating or cooling season.

Q: What is your typical turnaround time for repairs?

We understand that HVAC issues can arise unexpectedly. Our goal is to diagnose and resolve problems promptly. We will provide you with an estimated timeline during our assessment.

Q: Do you offer energy efficiency assessments?

Yes, we can conduct energy audits to identify opportunities for improving your property's energy efficiency, which often includes recommendations for HVAC upgrades.

Q: Are your technicians licensed and insured?

Absolutely. All our technicians are fully licensed, certified, and carry valid insurance coverage to ensure safe and worry-free service for our Dartmouth customers.
