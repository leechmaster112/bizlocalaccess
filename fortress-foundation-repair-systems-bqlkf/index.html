Common Foundation Issues in Rockwall

Foundations in Rockwall, TX, can face several challenges due to the local soil conditions and climate. Our team is well-equipped to handle:

• Heaving: This occurs when expansive clay soil absorbs too much water and expands, potentially lifting foundation slabs. We assess the extent and recommend appropriate slab leveling or stabilization techniques.

• Settling: As soil naturally compacts over time, foundations can settle, leading to cracks and misalignment. Our foundation repair methods effectively correct settlement issues at the source.

• Hydrostatic Pressure: Excess water in the soil around your foundation creates outward pressure, leading to cracks and potential water entry. We implement effective drainage solutions and waterproofing to combat this.

• Poor Drainage: Inadequate drainage around your home can saturate the soil and undermine your foundation. We analyze drainage patterns and recommend grading adjustments or drainage system installation.

Understanding these common Rockwall-specific foundation problems allows us to provide targeted and effective repair solutions for homeowners at 1001 N Goliad St.
