Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

1. Do you offer different themes or custom props for the photobooth?

Yes, we offer a wide range of standard props and themes. While we have many options available, we also accommodate custom requests to match your event's specific theme or color scheme. Please mention any specific prop or theme ideas when booking to see if they are possible.

2. How long does the setup and breakdown take for the photobooth?

Typically, our team requires approximately 1-2 hours for full setup and breakdown depending on the location and complexity of the event. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your event planning schedule.

3. Is the price listed on your website fixed regardless of event duration?

Yes, absolutely! Our pricing structure is straightforward. The quoted price for your photobooth rental remains the same whether your event lasts 2 hours or 10 hours. This fixed pricing model ensures you know exactly what you're paying for, without hidden fees based on time.

4. Do guests need to purchase props or prints?

No, guests do not need to purchase props or prints to use the photobooth. Standard props are included with the rental. While guests can purchase prints directly from the machine (if equipped), it's not mandatory. We provide a selection of props for free use.

5. Is a deposit required, and what does the rental package include?

We do not typically require a deposit unless specific custom props or extensive delivery distances are involved. Our standard rental package includes the photobooth unit, power hook-up, a selection of props, a dedicated operator for the duration of your event, and delivery and setup. Please check our specific package details or contact us for the most accurate information based on your event needs.
