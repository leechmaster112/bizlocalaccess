North 7th Street Church of Christ

We warmly invite you to join us in worship, fellowship, and spiritual growth at the North 7th Street Church of Christ, a welcoming Christian congregation in the heart of Harlingen. Our hearts and doors are open to all who seek to deepen their faith, grow closer to God, and study the Bible. As a vibrant community dedicated to New Testament restoration, we emulate the purity, unity, and devotion of the early church. Our mission is to foster a sacred space for worship services, Bible study, and Christian fellowship, where believers can connect meaningfully with God’s Word and one another. Join us for Sunday worship, prayer, and community outreach as we strive to live out the teachings of Jesus Christ and build a faithful, loving church family committed to spiritual growth and service.

Location

Welcome to the North 7th Street Church of Christ, a community of believers dedicated to serving God and supporting one another right here in our neighborhood. Located at 2205 N 7th St, Harlingen, TX 78550, our church is committed to providing a welcoming environment for worship, fellowship, and spiritual growth.

At the North 7th Street Church of Christ, we believe in the power of gathering together to worship God through music, prayer, and the study of His Word. Our services are designed to be meaningful and accessible to everyone, regardless of age or background. We strive to create a space where you can experience the love of Christ and grow in your relationship with Him.

Our primary focus is on the teachings of Jesus Christ and the importance of living a Christ-centered life. We offer regular opportunities for Bible study, prayer, and fellowship, ensuring that every member of our congregation feels valued and supported. Whether you are new to the faith or have been following Christ for many years, you are invited to join us as we seek to glorify God together.

Our services at 2205 N 7th St, Harlingen, TX 78550 are typically held on Sundays, starting at 10:00 AM. During the service, you can expect traditional hymns and contemporary worship music, a sermon based on Scripture, and opportunities for prayer and response. We encourage active participation and believe that worship is best done together as a community.

Alongside our main worship services, the North 7th Street Church of Christ offers various programs throughout the week to support spiritual growth and fellowship. These include small group Bible studies, prayer meetings, and community outreach initiatives. We believe that following Christ involves not only personal devotion but also serving others in practical ways within our local community.

Our building at 2205 N 7th St, Harlingen, TX 78550 is equipped with comfortable seating, accessible restrooms, and a welcoming atmosphere. We are committed to providing a safe and inclusive space for all who seek to learn about Christ or deepen their faith. Children are welcome during all services, and we offer childcare options to accommodate families.

Connect with us online through our website, https://harlingenchurch.com/, where you can find more information about our services, ministries, and upcoming events. You can also reach us directly by phone at (956) 320-7755 to ask questions or schedule a visit. We look forward to the opportunity to welcome you to our church family.

Join us at the North 7th Street Church of Christ for a meaningful worship experience and to be part of a community that cares for one another and serves God with joy and purpose.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When are the services held at the North 7th Street Church of Christ?

A: Our main worship services are held every Sunday beginning at 10:00 AM at our location, 2205 N 7th St, Harlingen, TX 78550.

Q: What can I expect during a typical service?

A: You can expect worship through music, a sermon based on Scripture, prayer opportunities, and fellowship with other members. Children are welcome and cared for during the service.

Q: How can I learn more about joining the church?

A: You can visit our website at https://harlingenchurch.com/ or call us at (956) 320-7755 to speak with a member of our congregation about how to connect with us.
