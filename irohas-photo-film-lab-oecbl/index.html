Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What film formats do you process at your Haymarket location?

We process a wide range of 35mm film formats, including both colour negative and black and white. We also handle medium format film (4x5, 645, 6x6, 6x7, Mamiya 6/7, Pentax 645D etc.) and roll film (110, 120, 220). Please specify the film type and format when placing your order.

2. How long does it typically take to process film at Irohas Photo Film Lab?

Standard film processing turnaround time from drop-off to collection is typically 3-5 business days for 35mm film. Medium format film usually takes 5-7 business days. Rush processing options may be available, subject to capacity and additional fees. Please check our website for the most current processing times.

3. Do you offer film scanning services at your Sydney CBD lab?

Yes, we offer professional film scanning services for your developed film rolls. We provide high-resolution scans of your negatives and slides, suitable for digital archiving and sharing. You can request this service when you drop off your film for processing.

4. Can I order prints along with my film processing?

Absolutely. We offer a variety of print options for your developed film. You can choose from different paper types, sizes, and black and white or colour printing. Specify your print requirements when placing your order via our website.

5. Is Irohas Photo Film Lab open to customers from other parts of Sydney?

While our physical location is at 398-402 Sussex St, Haymarket, NSW, 2000, our online services allow customers from anywhere to order film processing and scanning. However, our primary focus and service description here is for customers located at this specific address in Haymarket.
