Comprehensive Dental Care for All Ages

Whether you are a child establishing your dental routine, a young adult navigating orthodontic options, or a senior seeking comfortable and effective dental solutions, our team at Harewood Dental Clinic is equipped to provide personalized care. We believe that excellent oral health is foundational to overall well-being, and we take pride in our ability to deliver thorough and compassionate dental services to every member of your family.

Our Services Include:

Dental Examinations and Cleanings: Regular check-ups and professional cleanings are essential for maintaining healthy teeth and gums. Our skilled dental hygienists and dentists perform thorough examinations to detect any potential issues early, ensuring prompt and effective treatment.

Fillings and Restorations: We offer durable and aesthetic solutions for damaged or decayed teeth, including composite fillings and other restorative procedures to restore your smile and function.

Crowns and Bridges: For more extensive restorations, our clinic provides custom-crafted crowns and bridges to restore damaged teeth and improve your ability to chew comfortably.

Root Canal Therapy: Advanced dental technology allows us to perform effective root canal treatments, saving infected teeth and alleviating pain with precision and care.

Dental Implants: Missing teeth can impact your quality of life, but our implant services offer a permanent solution for restoring both function and appearance.

Oral Surgery: When complex procedures are necessary, our clinic provides expert oral surgery services under local anesthesia, ensuring safety and comfort for our patients.

Emergency Dental Care: We understand that dental emergencies can arise unexpectedly. While our regular hours are for scheduled appointments, we are committed to addressing urgent dental issues promptly, often offering guidance or emergency treatment options for our Nanaimo patients.

Modern Technology and Patient Comfort

We invest in state-of-the-art dental technology to enhance the accuracy and efficiency of our treatments. From digital X-rays to advanced imaging, we ensure that your care is both effective and minimally invasive. Our modern clinic is designed with your comfort in mind, featuring ergonomic dental chairs, reclining positions, and a stress-free atmosphere to make your visit as comfortable as possible.

Commitment to Your Oral Health

At Harewood Dental Clinic, we prioritize patient education and preventive care. We believe that informed patients are empowered patients, and we take the time to explain your treatment options, address any concerns, and provide practical tips for maintaining excellent oral hygiene at home. Our goal is not just to treat dental issues but to help you achieve and maintain a healthy, beautiful smile for life.
