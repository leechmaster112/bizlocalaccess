Location

Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care - Huntersville

Welcoming you and your beloved pets to our state-of-the-art veterinary clinic located at 14227 Boren St Suite 100, Huntersville, NC 28078. As the first Petfolk facility in this community, we are dedicated exclusively to providing exceptional, compassionate care right here in your neighborhood.

Our expert team of veterinarians and technicians is committed to delivering the highest standard of medical care for cats and dogs. We understand that your pets are cherished members of the family, and we approach each visit with the care and attention they deserve.

At our Huntersville location, we offer a comprehensive range of veterinary services designed to keep your furry friends healthy and happy. Our focus includes:

Wellness Examinations: Regular check-ups are crucial for early detection of potential health issues and maintaining your pet's optimal health. Vaccinations: Keeping your pet's vaccinations up-to-date is vital for preventing serious and potentially life-threatening diseases. Dental Care: Professional cleaning, extractions, and advice on at-home dental care to ensure your pet's oral health. Laboratory Testing: On-site diagnostics allow for quick analysis of blood, urine, and other samples to aid in diagnosis. Urinary Catheterization: A critical service for pets requiring temporary urinary assistance. IV Fluid Therapy: Essential for treating dehydration, aiding recovery, or managing chronic conditions. Fracture Care: Expert handling and stabilization for broken bones. Soft Tissue Surgery: Procedures for conditions affecting internal organs and structures. And much more, including parasite control and minor wound care.

Our facility is equipped with modern technology and staffed by experienced professionals who prioritize your pet's comfort and well-being. We pride ourselves on providing thorough, personalized care in a clean and welcoming environment.

For more information about our services or to schedule an appointment, please visit our website or contact us directly.
