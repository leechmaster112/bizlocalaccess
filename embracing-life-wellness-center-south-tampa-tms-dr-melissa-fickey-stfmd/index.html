FAQ

What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your South Tampa location?

TMS, or Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation, is an FDA-cleared, non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our specific office at 3333 W Kennedy Blvd #106, you will recline in a comfortable chair while an electromagnetic coil is gently placed against your scalp. The treatment is administered by our trained staff under the supervision of Dr. Fickey, and you are awake and alert throughout the entire session.

How do I know if I am a candidate for TMS with Dr. Melissa Fickey?

The best way to determine if TMS therapy is right for you is to schedule a consultation at our Tampa wellness center. Dr. Fickey will conduct a comprehensive assessment to understand your specific history and needs. TMS is often recommended for individuals who have not experienced satisfactory results from traditional antidepressant medications.

What can I expect during a typical treatment session at your Tampa clinic?

Each TMS session at our location on W Kennedy Blvd typically lasts about 20-30 minutes. After checking in, you will be seated in one of our treatment rooms. You may hear a clicking sound and feel a light tapping sensation on your head during the procedure. Many patients choose to relax, listen to music, or simply rest during their treatment before returning to their daily routine immediately afterward.
