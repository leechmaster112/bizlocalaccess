Joanna N. Rosenblatt, LCSW

Joanna N. Rosenblatt, LCSW, is a licensed clinical social worker providing compassionate, trauma-informed mental health therapy in Pleasantville, NY, with teletherapy available in New York and Connecticut. She specializes in helping adults navigate complex emotional challenges, including trauma recovery, obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), and perinatal mental health concerns such as pregnancy-related stress, postpartum anxiety, and fertility-related emotional impacts. Joanna integrates evidence-based practices, including EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing), CBT, mindfulness, and holistic therapeutic approaches tailored to each individual’s needs. Her work supports lasting healing by addressing intrusive thoughts, unresolved past experiences, and emotional overwhelm in a confidential, collaborative, and supportive environment. Joanna offers both in-person and online therapy to adults seeking clarity, resilience, and meaningful change.
