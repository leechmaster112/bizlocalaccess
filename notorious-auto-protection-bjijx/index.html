Our Paint Protection Film service involves applying a clear, urethane-based film to vulnerable areas of your car's exterior. This invisible shield protects against rock chips, scratches from debris, bug splatter, and harsh weather conditions. The film is custom-cut to your vehicle's contours, providing a seamless finish that maintains the factory paint integrity. We use only top-tier PPF products from trusted manufacturers to ensure maximum durability and clarity.

Complementing our PPF service is our Ceramic Coating application. This liquid polymer creates a hydrophobic barrier over your paint, causing water and contaminants to bead and easily wash away. The result is easier maintenance, enhanced gloss, and superior protection against UV damage and environmental contaminants. Our ceramic coating process includes thorough surface preparation and multiple layers for optimal adhesion and longevity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How long does the PPF application process typically take?

A: The PPF installation process usually takes 1-2 days for a full vehicle, depending on the complexity and size of the vehicle. We schedule appointments accordingly to minimize inconvenience.

Q2: How long does the ceramic coating last?

A: With proper maintenance, ceramic coating can last anywhere from 2 to 5 years. The longevity depends on factors like climate, driving conditions, and how well the coating is maintained.

Q3: Can I drive my car immediately after PPF installation?

A: Yes, you can drive your vehicle immediately after PPF installation. The film is designed to be durable and road-ready right after application.

Q4: What's the difference between PPF and ceramic coating?

A: Paint Protection Film is a physical barrier that shields your paint from damage, while ceramic coating creates a chemical bond that repels contaminants and enhances gloss. Both offer protection but in different ways.

Q5: Do you offer mobile services for PPF or ceramic coating in Syracuse?

A: Currently, we only offer services at our Syracuse location at 6742 Pickard Dr. We recommend scheduling a visit to our facility for the best results.
