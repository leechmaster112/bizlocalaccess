Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: How often should I get my air ducts cleaned?

Generally, most experts recommend professional air duct cleaning at least once every 3 to 5 years for typical homes. However, factors specific to your Dallas home or business, such as recent renovations, significant dust accumulation, pet ownership, or if someone in the household has allergies, might warrant more frequent cleaning.

Q2: What exactly does an air duct cleaning involve?

Our process typically includes a visual inspection, then using powerful rotary and vacuum systems to dislodge and remove debris from the duct walls, registers, and grilles. We also clean the interior of your HVAC unit's components to ensure optimal performance and air quality.

Q3: How long does a typical duct cleaning job take?

The duration depends on the size of the ductwork, the extent of the buildup, and the number of accessible vents. On average, a standard residential cleaning at our Dallas location can take anywhere from 2 to 4 hours to complete thoroughly.

Q4: Will cleaning my ducts help with my allergies?

Absolutely. Air ducts can harbor significant amounts of dust, pollen, pet dander, mold spores, and other allergens. Professional cleaning removes these irritants from the air stream, significantly improving indoor air quality and potentially alleviating allergy symptoms for Dallas residents.

Q5: Is there any preparation needed before your team arrives?

While our team is equipped to handle most situations, it's helpful if you can clear space around your main HVAC unit (usually located in the attic, crawlspace, or utility closet) for easy access. We'll also provide instructions regarding temporarily closing certain vents if necessary for the specific cleaning process.
