Services Provided at 410 N Center Dr

We offer a variety of evidence-based services tailored to meet the diverse needs of our Norfolk clients:

Counseling and Therapy

Our licensed therapists provide individual, group, and family counseling focused on mental health, emotional well-being, coping skills, and personal growth. We address a wide range of concerns including depression, anxiety, stress management, grief, and relationship issues, all within the supportive walls of our Norfolk center.

Support Groups

We facilitate numerous support groups for individuals and families facing specific challenges. These groups offer a safe space to share experiences, learn from others, and build a network of understanding and encouragement specific to the Norfolk community.

Case Management

Our case managers work closely with clients to assess needs, develop personalized plans, connect with community resources, and ensure coordinated support. This service is crucial for navigating complex situations and accessing necessary assistance while remaining connected to the Norfolk area.

Psychosocial Rehabilitation

Our programs help individuals develop essential life skills, improve social functioning, and enhance their ability to live independently or participate fully in community life. These services are delivered right here in Norfolk, empowering clients to achieve their personal goals.

Community Integration Activities

We believe in the power of connection. Our staff organizes activities and supports participation in community events, helping clients build relationships and integrate successfully into the fabric of Norfolk.
