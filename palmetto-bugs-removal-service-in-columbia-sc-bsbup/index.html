Understanding the Palmetto Bug

Often referred to simply as "palmetto bugs," these are the large, reddish-brown cockroaches common to the Carolinas. While less likely to carry diseases than smaller species, they are significant nuisances. They thrive in dark, damp environments and can quickly become a problem in basements, crawl spaces, under sinks, and anywhere there's access to food and water. Their size and ability to squeeze through small gaps make them particularly challenging to control without professional intervention.
