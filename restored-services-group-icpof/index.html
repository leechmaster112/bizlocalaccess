Understanding the Restoration Process

Water damage restoration is a complex process that requires expertise, specialized equipment, and a systematic approach. At Restored Services Group, we follow a comprehensive methodology to ensure thorough and effective restoration:

1. Emergency Assessment and Water Removal: Upon arrival, our certified technicians conduct a rapid assessment to determine the source and extent of the water damage. The immediate priority is to remove standing water using powerful extraction equipment. This step is crucial to prevent the spread of water to unaffected areas and to begin drying the structure.

2. Drying and Dehumidification: Following water removal, the drying process begins in earnest. We utilize advanced industrial-grade dehumidifiers and air movers strategically placed throughout the affected area. These units effectively remove residual moisture from surfaces and the air, preventing mold and mildew proliferation. We employ moisture detection technology to monitor progress and ensure complete drying is achieved, even in hidden areas like wall cavities and under flooring.

3. Structural and Surface Cleaning: Once the structure is adequately dried, we proceed with cleaning. This involves removing contaminated debris, sanitizing affected surfaces, and preparing the area for repairs or refinishing. Our cleaning solutions are designed to eliminate odors and residue left behind by water and contaminants.

4. Mold Prevention and Remediation (if necessary): We are vigilant about mold prevention during the drying process. If mold growth is detected or suspected, our team is fully equipped and trained to perform professional mold remediation according to industry standards, ensuring the health and safety of occupants.

5. Reconstruction and Restoration: The final stage involves restoring your property to its pre-loss condition. This may include repairing damaged drywall, flooring, cabinetry, or other structural elements. We work closely with you to ensure the repairs meet your expectations and restore functionality and aesthetics.
