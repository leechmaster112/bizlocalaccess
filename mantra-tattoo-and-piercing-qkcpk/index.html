Q3: What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, all major credit cards, and debit cards. Please call (720) 639-6333 for the most current payment policy regarding deposits and full payment.

Q4: Do you offer touch-ups?

Yes, we offer complimentary touch-up services for Mantra clients within the first year after the initial procedure, provided the aftercare instructions were followed diligently.
