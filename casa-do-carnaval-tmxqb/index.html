Welcome to Casa do Carnaval, the vibrant heart of Portuguese Carnaval celebrations right here in the heart of New York! We are dedicated to bringing the energy, color, and joy of the world's most famous pre-Lenten festival directly to the Big Apple. Our mission is to offer New Yorkers and visitors an unparalleled experience of the authentic Portuguese Carnaval, creating a unique cultural hub where tradition meets the excitement of the city.
