Location

Petfolk Veterinary & Urgent Care - Southside

Located at 4852 Town Center Pkwy Suite 108, Jacksonville, FL 32246, Petfolk's first Southside veterinary clinic provides expert care for your beloved pets. Our dedicated team of veterinarians and technicians creates a welcoming environment focused on preventive care, urgent treatment, and long-term wellness solutions.

Comprehensive Local Services

At our Southside location, we offer a wide range of veterinary services tailored to meet the needs of Jacksonville pet owners. Our comprehensive approach ensures your pets receive the best care right here in our community.

Wellness Exams & Preventive Care

Regular check-ups are essential for maintaining your pet's health. Our veterinarians conduct thorough examinations, recommend appropriate vaccinations, and develop personalized wellness plans to keep your furry friends healthy and happy.

Urgent Care Services

Our urgent care facility handles minor to moderate pet emergencies. From lacerations and sprains to ingestion of toxins, our team is equipped to provide prompt, professional care when your pet needs it most.

Dental Care

We offer comprehensive dental services including teeth cleaning, extractions, and oral health guidance. Healthy teeth and gums are crucial for overall pet wellness, and our Southside clinic provides state-of-the-art dental care.

Laboratory Services

Our on-site laboratory allows for rapid diagnostic testing, including bloodwork and urinalysis. This enables us to quickly identify health concerns and develop effective treatment plans for your pets.

Emergency Preparedness

While we focus on routine care, our team is trained to handle unexpected health issues. We stock emergency medications and equipment to address critical situations that may arise with your pets.

Community Focus

As your local veterinary partner, we're committed to building lasting relationships with Jacksonville pet owners. Our Southside location serves as a resource for pet owners seeking reliable, compassionate care for their companions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What are your regular business hours?

Answer: Our Southside clinic operates from 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM Monday through Friday, and from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM on Saturdays. We are closed on Sundays to dedicate time to professional development and ensure our team is fully prepared for the next day.

Q2: Do you offer pet boarding services?

Answer: Currently, we focus exclusively on veterinary care at our 4852 Town Center Pkwy location. We recommend consulting with us about local boarding options that align with your pet's specific needs.

Q3: How quickly can you address after-hours emergencies?

Answer: While we're closed to the public after hours, our team has emergency protocols in place. For urgent concerns, please call our number during business hours, and we'll provide guidance or arrange for appropriate care.

Q4: Are payment plans available for major procedures?

Answer: Yes, we offer payment plan options for eligible services. Discuss your financial needs with our front desk staff when scheduling appointments or at the time of service.

Q5: Do you provide pet grooming services?

Answer: Our Southside location focuses on medical care. We can recommend excellent local grooming salons that accept your pets and coordinate care with our veterinary services if needed.
