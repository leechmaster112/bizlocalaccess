Melby Electric

Melby Electric is a full-service electrical contractor based in Marysville, Washington, that serves residential and commercial clients throughout Snohomish County and the surrounding regions. Founded in 2020 by local expert Jake Melby, the company is built on a foundation of reliability, high-quality workmanship, and a strong commitment to customer satisfaction. They aim to build lasting trust with their clients by providing honest and professional electrical solutions for projects of any size. The company offers a comprehensive range of electrical services to meet the needs of any home or business. Their expertise includes general electrical repairs, such as fixing outlets and switches, as well as major installations like new lighting fixtures, ceiling fans, and complete property rewires. They also specialize in critical system upgrades, including electrical panel and circuit breaker replacements to ensure safety and efficiency. Furthermore, Melby Electric is a certified Generac dealer, providing sales, installation, and maintenance for home standby generators, and they also handle modern installations like EV charging stations and home automation systems. Serving their home base of Marysville, Melby Electric's service area extends across all of Snohomish County, reaching into South Skagit and North King counties as well. This broad coverage allows them to assist a wide range of customers with everything from small household repairs to significant commercial electrical work. Recognizing that electrical issues can arise unexpectedly, the company also offers 24/7 emergency services to provide prompt and reliable assistance whenever it is needed, solidifying their reputation as a dependable local contractor.
