Our Core Service: Timber Deck Restoration

Our primary offering is a thorough deck restoration process. This isn't merely about cleaning; it's a multi-step procedure designed to address the underlying issues affecting your deck's health and appearance.

1. Assessment: We begin with a detailed inspection of your deck. We evaluate the condition of the timber, checking for structural soundness, identifying any rot or damage, assessing the level of staining, and understanding the extent of wear. This initial step is crucial for tailoring our approach to your specific deck's needs.

2. Cleaning & Decontamination: We employ specialized techniques and environmentally conscious cleaning solutions to remove deeply embedded dirt, grease, oil stains, moss, mildew, algae, and pollen. This step is vital not only for aesthetics but also for preparing the surface properly for the next stages, ensuring the best adhesion for protective treatments.

3. Sanding & Refinishing (Optional): Depending on the condition and your preferences, we may lightly sand the surface to remove rough patches and open the wood pores. This prepares the deck for optimal absorption of our rejuvenating oils and finishes.

4. Rejuvenating Treatment: This is the heart of our service. We apply high-quality, penetrating oils and finishes specifically designed for timber decks. These products are chosen for their ability to deeply condition the wood, enhance its natural grain and colour, and provide robust protection against further damage from UV rays, moisture, and general environmental factors. We focus on treatments that allow the wood to breathe while providing a durable barrier.

5. Protective Coating: We apply a protective topcoat designed to shield the deck from harsh weather conditions, foot traffic, and UV degradation. This coating enhances durability and helps maintain the refreshed appearance for an extended period.

Our process is meticulous and tailored. We believe in using products that are effective yet kind to the environment, ensuring the health of your deck and the surrounding area.
