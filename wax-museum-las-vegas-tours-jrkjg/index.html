Location

Welcome to Wax Museum Las Vegas Tours, located at 3377 Las Vegas Blvd S, Las Vegas, NV 89109. We are proud to be the most Instagrammable wax museum in the heart of Las Vegas, offering an unparalleled experience for visitors seeking a unique blend of celebrity admiration and interactive fun.

Step into our meticulously crafted museum and be captivated by our extensive collection of larger-than-life wax figures representing some of the world's most iconic celebrities. From legendary musicians and actors to groundbreaking inventors and cultural icons, our figures are designed with remarkable detail to provide an immersive experience.

Our interactive photo ops are a highlight for many visitors. Pose alongside your favorite stars, recreate famous moments, or simply capture your own unique expressions for memories that will last a lifetime. The vibrant, themed environments surrounding each figure enhance the realism and add another layer of engagement to your visit.

For groups of all sizes, we offer tailored tours that showcase the museum's highlights. Our knowledgeable guides bring the exhibits to life with fascinating facts and stories, making for an informative and entertaining experience. Whether you're a history buff, a celebrity enthusiast, or simply looking for a fun outing, our tours cater to everyone's interests.

Located just steps away from the dazzling Las Vegas Strip, our museum provides a unique contrast to the city's typical entertainment landscape. We offer a memorable, educational, and visually stunning experience that appeals to all ages. Plan your visit to 3377 Las Vegas Blvd S and discover why our wax museum is considered one of the most popular attractions in Las Vegas.

Question: What are the typical tour times at the Wax Museum Las Vegas?

Answer: Tours generally run every 30 minutes, with sessions available from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM daily. We recommend checking our website for specific times on holidays or during special events.

Question: Can I purchase tickets for a group larger than 15 people?

Answer: Yes, we accommodate groups of any size. For groups larger than 15, please call our office directly at (702) 555-9943 to arrange a private tour and discuss any specific requirements.

Question: Are there any age restrictions for visiting the museum?

Answer: The museum is suitable for all ages. Our exhibits and tours are designed to be engaging for children and adults alike.

Question: Can I bring my own props for photos?

Answer: Personal props are welcome for general photography within the museum. Please note that due to safety and preservation concerns, props used for close-up interaction with figures may be restricted.

Question: Is the museum accessible for visitors with disabilities?

Answer: Yes, our museum is fully accessible. We have wheelchair ramps throughout the facility, accessible restrooms, and staff available to assist with any mobility needs.
