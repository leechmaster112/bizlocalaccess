The Real Skin Medical Center

The Real Skin Medical Center is a premium medical spa located in Costa Mesa, California, dedicated to helping clients look and feel their best through medical-grade aesthetic treatments. Led by Director Dr. Calvin Doan and expert injector Paveena Doan, NP, our team takes a "Skin First" approach — prioritizing your skin's long-term health over quick fixes. We believe it's not about looking different, but about looking rested, refreshed, and confident. Our services include injectables and fillers (Botox, Dysport, and dermal fillers), biostimulators such as Sculptra to restore natural collagen, advanced skin and hair treatments including Glo2 facials, and wellness injections like B12 and NAD+. Every treatment is customized to your unique goals and delivered with a meticulous, artistic hand. We are open by appointment only and serve clients throughout Orange County and the greater Southern California area. Book online at therealskin.co and discover your skin, but better.
