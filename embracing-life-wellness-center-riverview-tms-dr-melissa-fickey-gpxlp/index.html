FAQ

1. What is TMS therapy and how is it performed at your Riverview clinic?

TMS, or Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation, is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression. At our Riverview location, you will sit comfortably in a chair while a specialized coil is placed gently against your head. The treatment is administered right here in our office at 6332 US-301 and typically lasts around 20 minutes. You are awake and alert during the entire process and can drive yourself home or back to work immediately afterward.

2. Who is a good candidate for TMS treatment at Embracing Life Wellness Center?

TMS therapy is typically recommended for adults who have been diagnosed with Major Depressive Disorder and have not experienced satisfactory improvement from previous antidepressant medications. The best way to determine if you are a candidate is to schedule a consultation with Dr. Melissa Fickey at our Riverview clinic for a comprehensive evaluation of your specific situation and health history.

3. What other services do you offer besides TMS at your US-301 location?

In addition to TMS therapy, our Riverview clinic provides a range of psychiatric services including detailed psychiatric evaluations, ongoing medication management, and supportive psychotherapy. All of our services are designed to work together to create a holistic and personalized treatment plan for your mental wellness.

4. How do I schedule an appointment at your Riverview office?

Scheduling an appointment is simple. You can reach our office directly by calling (813) 662-5919. Our team will guide you through the process and answer any initial questions you may have about beginning your care at our location on 6332 US-301.

5. Do you accept insurance for treatments at your clinic?

We work with various insurance providers to make our services accessible. Coverage for TMS therapy and other treatments can vary. Our administrative staff at the Riverview clinic will verify your insurance benefits and discuss all financial aspects with you before you begin your treatment plan.
