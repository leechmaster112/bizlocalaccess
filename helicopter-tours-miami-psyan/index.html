Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What types of tours do you offer in New York?

We primarily offer scenic sightseeing tours over New York City, focusing on iconic landmarks such as the Statue of Liberty, Brooklyn Bridge, and the Manhattan skyline. We also offer specialized tours for special occasions like weddings and corporate events.

Q2: How long does a standard New York tour last?

A typical New York City sightseeing tour lasts approximately 25-30 minutes, providing ample time to see the major highlights. Special occasion tours can be customized to your desired duration.

Q3: Is there a weight limit for passengers?

Yes, for safety reasons, our tours have a weight limit of approximately 300 pounds (136 kg) per passenger. Children under a certain weight may fly with an adult or require a child restraint system, depending on the aircraft model.

Q4: What is the cancellation policy?

We require 24 hours' notice for cancellations or rescheduling. If you cancel with less than 24 hours' notice, the full tour price will be forfeited. In case of inclement weather that forces flight cancellations, we will reschedule your tour free of charge.

Q5: Are the helicopters equipped with safety features?

Absolutely. All our helicopters are equipped with state-of-the-art safety features, including GPS flight recorders, emergency locator beacons, and advanced communication systems. Our pilots are highly trained and certified by the FAA, and our aircraft undergo regular maintenance according to strict safety standards.
