Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) - New York Services

1. Question: What airports in New York do you operate out of?

Answer: We primarily operate out of John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) and LaGuardia Airport (LGA) in New York City. Depending on your specific needs and aircraft type, we may also coordinate arrivals and departures from Teterboro Airport (TEB) or other nearby regional airports.

2. Question: How far in advance should I book a private jet flight to/from New York?

Answer: While we strive for same-day availability for New York, we recommend booking at least 24-48 hours in advance for guaranteed aircraft availability, especially during peak travel times or for larger groups. For international flights or special requests, booking well ahead is advised.

3. Question: What types of aircraft are available for charter in New York?

Answer: We offer a diverse fleet suitable for New York travel, including spacious cabin jets like the Citation X, Challenger 300, and Gulfstream models, as well as versatile turboprops like the King Air and Beechcraft. We can tailor the aircraft to your passenger count, luggage requirements, and desired level of comfort.

4. Question: Do you handle customs clearance for international flights arriving/departing from New York?

Answer: Yes, as part of our comprehensive service, our team manages all customs and immigration procedures for international flights originating from or arriving at New York airports (JFK, LGA, ETE, etc.), ensuring a smooth and efficient process.

5. Question: What is the typical turnaround time for a private jet at JFK or LGA?

Answer: Our goal is to minimize turnaround time for a seamless experience. Depending on the flight schedule and airport traffic, we typically aim for a prompt turn-around, allowing for a comfortable wait between flights if necessary, or facilitating a quick connection.
