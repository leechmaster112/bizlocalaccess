Understanding NeuroStar TMS Therapy

NeuroStar TMS is a non-invasive procedure that uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain's prefrontal cortex, a region often associated with mood regulation. Unlike older forms of TMS, NeuroStar utilizes a patented Dynamic Field Technology® (DFT®) that automatically adjusts the magnetic pulses in real-time. This technology ensures that the stimulation is precisely targeted and individually optimized for each patient's unique brain anatomy, maximizing potential therapeutic effects and minimizing discomfort.

The treatment itself is completely painless and does not require any anesthesia or sedation. During a typical session, you will sit comfortably in a chair, wearing ear protection, as the TMS device generates a gentle tapping sound and mild tingling sensation on the scalp where the coil is placed. Our trained technicians at our Franklin location will guide you through each session, ensuring your comfort and safety throughout the 20- to 40-minute procedure.

The TMS Treatment Process at Our Franklin Clinic

Your journey with TMS at our Franklin location typically involves several phases:

1. Initial Consultation and Screening: We begin with a thorough assessment to understand your medical history, current symptoms, and treatment history.

2. Comprehensive Evaluation: We conduct detailed brain imaging (typically an MRI) to map your brain's structure and determine the optimal placement for the TMS coil.

3. TMS Treatment Protocol: You will typically receive sessions five days a week (Monday through Friday) for approximately four to six weeks. Each session is brief, lasting about 20-40 minutes.

4. Ongoing Assessment: Throughout your treatment course, our team will monitor your progress and adjust the protocol as needed.

5. Follow-Up and Maintenance: After completing the primary treatment phase, we discuss long-term management strategies to maintain your improved mood.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) about TMS of Tennessee (Franklin Location)

1. What is NeuroStar TMS Therapy?

NeuroStar TMS is a non-invasive treatment for depression that uses focused magnetic pulses to stimulate nerve cells in the brain's mood-regulating region. It is FDA-cleared and performed at our Franklin location.

2. Is TMS Therapy safe?

Yes, NeuroStar TMS Therapy is considered safe and well-tolerated. It does not require anesthesia, surgery, or electrode placement. The most common side effects are mild headache or scalp discomfort, which are typically temporary. Our team at our Franklin office prioritizes your safety throughout the procedure.

3. How long does a TMS session take?

Each individual TMS treatment session at our Franklin clinic typically lasts between 20 and 40 minutes. You will likely only need to spend about 45-60 minutes at our Franklin location for each appointment, including setup and brief monitoring.

4. How many TMS sessions are needed?

A typical course of NeuroStar TMS Therapy involves sessions five days a week for approximately four to six weeks. The exact number of sessions needed is determined during your initial evaluation at our Franklin practice.

5. Does TMS require anesthesia or medication?

No, NeuroStar TMS Therapy is completely non-invasive and does not require any anesthesia or sedation. You will remain awake and alert throughout your treatment at our Franklin location.
