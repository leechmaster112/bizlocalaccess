Q2: What types of water damage do you handle?

We handle all types of water damage, classified by the source:

Category 1 (Clean Water): From clean water sources like broken pipes or appliance leaks.

Category 2 (Gray Water): Contains significant contaminants, like from a dishwasher overflow or leaking washing machine.

Category 3 (Black Water): Highly contaminated water, typically from sewage backups.

Our team is fully equipped and trained to handle all categories safely and effectively.
