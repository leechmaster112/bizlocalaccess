Rosati's Pizza Pub and Sports Bar

Rosati's Pizza serves signature Chicago-style pizzas, pastas, wings, salads, and sandwiches that offer high quality, value and flavor. With five generations of experience, every piece of every recipe is original, ensuring Rosati's serves one-of-a-kind dishes from a one-of-a-kind family. As one of the oldest surviving family pizza brands in the U.S., the Rosati name has been firmly established in the city of Chicago and its suburbs. Generations have grown up with the brand and continue to spread it to every corner of the country. Transplanted Chicagoans all over the map have helped bring their favorite pizzeria to 150+ locations across twenty states.
