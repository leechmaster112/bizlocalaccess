Q: Is acupuncture painful?

Acupuncture involves the insertion of fine needles into specific points on the body. While there may be a slight sensation upon insertion, most patients find the experience to be comfortable or even relaxing. Pointe Acupuncture & Integrative Medicine uses only sterile, single-use needles to ensure safety and comfort for our patients in Grosse Pointe Park.
