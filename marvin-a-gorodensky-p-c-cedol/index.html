The timeline for employment cases in Toronto can vary significantly depending on the complexity of the case, the evidence involved, and court availability. Q3: Do I need a lawyer for a workplace complaint in Toronto?

While you have the right to represent yourself, employment law is complex, and employers or their representatives often have sophisticated legal teams. Having an experienced employment lawyer in Toronto on your side significantly increases your chances of achieving a fair outcome and ensures your rights are fully protected throughout the process.

Q4: What should I do immediately after experiencing workplace harassment in Toronto?

If you experience workplace harassment in Toronto, it is important to document everything. Keep a detailed record of the incidents, including dates, times, locations, what was said or done, and any witnesses. Report the harassment through your employer's formal procedures as outlined in your employee handbook or collective agreement. Contacting us promptly can help preserve evidence and guide you through the next steps.
