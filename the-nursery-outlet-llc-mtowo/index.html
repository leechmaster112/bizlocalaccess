The Nursery Outlet, LLC

The Nursery Outlet is a premier commercial and retail plant nursery located in Woodburn, Oregon, specializing in robust landscape essentials engineered for Pacific Northwest yards. We supply an extensive inventory of over 600 plant species at everyday wholesale outlet pricing, serving professional landscape contractors, designers, and residential shoppers throughout Marion County and the Willamette Valley.
