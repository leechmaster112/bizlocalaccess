Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How long does a typical carpet cleaning job take?

A: The duration of a carpet cleaning job depends on the size of the area and the level of soiling. On average, a standard living room or hallway takes between 1 to 3 hours. We always provide a specific timeframe during our free estimate.

Q2: Are your cleaning products safe for homes with children and pets?

A: Absolutely. We use only eco-friendly, non-toxic, and residue-free cleaning solutions that are safe for homes with children and pets. All products are thoroughly explained during our consultation.

Q3: Can you clean different types of carpets, like Berber or wool?

A: Yes, our experienced team is trained to handle various carpet types, including Berber, shag, and wool. We use specialized techniques and equipment appropriate for each specific carpet fiber.

Q4: Do you offer emergency carpet cleaning services?

A: Yes, we understand accidents happen. We offer emergency services for spills and urgent cleaning needs. Contact us immediately for prompt assistance.

Q5: How often should I schedule professional carpet cleaning?

A: For most homes, professional carpet cleaning is recommended at least once or twice a year. High-traffic areas or homes with children/pets may benefit from more frequent cleanings.
