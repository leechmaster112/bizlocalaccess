Greg Sheena Law

Greg Sheena Law is a Southfield-based law firm focused on protecting people facing criminal, traffic, and high-stress legal problems across Metro Detroit. From first-time misdemeanors and speeding tickets to serious felony charges and complex family law disputes, attorney Gregory R. Sheena delivers calm, strategic representation when the stakes feel overwhelming. Based in Southfield at 29500 Telegraph Rd Suite 2500, Greg Sheena Law works daily in courts throughout Oakland, Macomb, and Wayne County. Clients turn to the firm for help with criminal defense, traffic tickets, OWI/DUIs, driver’s license issues, domestic violence charges, and other serious accusations that can threaten freedom, driving privileges, and employment. The firm also handles select divorce and family law matters, providing steady guidance in cases that affect children, finances, and long-term stability. What sets Greg Sheena Law apart is personal attention and straightforward communication. Every case is reviewed in detail, with a clear plan built around the client’s priorities—reducing charges, protecting a record, avoiding jail, or fighting the case at trial when necessary. Clients get direct access to their attorney, prompt updates, and honest expectations at every stage of the process. If you’ve been arrested, cited, or served with papers in Metro Detroit, you don’t have to face the system alone. Greg Sheena Law in Southfield is committed to protecting your rights, explaining your options in plain language, and pushing for the best possible outcome—whether that means a reduction, dismissal, or strong defense in court.
