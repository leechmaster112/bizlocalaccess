FAQs:

Q: What should I do immediately after a car accident in Flint?

After ensuring safety, gather information (names, contact details, insurance info, vehicle details) from all involved parties and any witnesses. Report the accident to the police and seek medical attention even if you feel fine initially.

Q: How do I know if I have a strong car accident case in Michigan?

We evaluate the strength of your case by assessing liability, the extent of your injuries, medical treatment required, and the impact on your life. Factors like traffic violations, insurance coverage, and comparative negligence are considered.

Q: How much does it cost to consult with your Flint car accident lawyers?

We offer free initial consultations. Most car accident cases in Michigan are handled on a contingency fee basis, meaning you pay nothing upfront, and our fee is only collected if we successfully secure compensation for you.

Q: How long do I have to file a claim after my Flint car accident?

In Michigan, the statute of limitations for personal injury claims, including car accidents, is typically 2 years from the date of the accident. It's crucial to consult with an attorney promptly.

Q: Will the insurance company contact me directly about my case?

Often, the insurance company will reach out, but they may try to settle quickly for a low amount. We recommend speaking with an attorney before providing recorded statements or signing any settlement documents.
