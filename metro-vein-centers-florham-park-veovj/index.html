Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of vein treatments do you offer at your Florham Park location?

Our Florham Park center provides a range of advanced vein treatments, including sclerotherapy, endovenous laser treatment (EVLT), radiofrequency ablation (RFA), and VeinGogh® treatment. These procedures are designed to effectively address spider veins, varicose veins, and other venous conditions.

Q2: How long does a typical vein treatment procedure take at your Florham Park clinic?

Most vein treatment procedures performed at our Florham Park location are relatively quick, typically lasting between 30 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the complexity of the case and the specific treatment being administered.

Q3: Is there any downtime associated with vein treatments at your Florham Park center?

Downtime varies depending on the procedure and individual healing factors. Many patients can return to their normal activities immediately after a treatment, while others may need a day or two of rest. Our team will provide specific guidance based on your treatment plan.

Q4: Do I need a referral to schedule a vein consultation at your Florham Park location?

While a referral is not always required, we welcome patients who may have been referred by their primary care physician. You can also schedule a consultation directly at our Florham Park center if you are experiencing vein concerns.

Q5: What should I expect during my first visit to Metro Vein Centers Florham Park?

During your initial visit, our experienced vascular physician will conduct a thorough examination, possibly including an ultrasound, to diagnose your condition. We will discuss your symptoms, medical history, and the most appropriate treatment options tailored to your specific needs. We'll answer all your questions and help you understand the recommended course of action.
