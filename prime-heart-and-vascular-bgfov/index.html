Our Cardiology Services

Our cardiology practice encompasses a broad spectrum of heart-related issues. We provide thorough evaluations for patients experiencing symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, palpitations, or dizziness. Our diagnostic capabilities include comprehensive stress testing, state-of-the-art echocardiography (ultrasound of the heart), and advanced cardiac imaging.

For established heart conditions, our cardiologists offer comprehensive management plans. This includes medication management for conditions like high blood pressure, high cholesterol, and heart failure, as well as guidance on lifestyle modifications to improve cardiovascular health. We also provide specialized care for arrhythmias, utilizing modern techniques including electrophysiology studies and appropriate interventions.

Our Vascular Services

Our vascular team specializes in the diagnosis and treatment of diseases affecting the blood vessels outside the heart. We address conditions such as peripheral artery disease, carotid artery disease, and venous disorders. Our approach includes thorough vascular assessments using techniques like ultrasound and, when necessary, advanced imaging.

We offer a range of minimally invasive procedures performed by experienced vascular specialists. These include procedures to clear blocked arteries (angioplasty and stenting), treat venous reflux (endovenous laser treatment or radiofrequency ablation), and manage aneurysms. Our commitment is to provide effective solutions with minimal discomfort and downtime for our Allen patients.

Preventative Care and Patient Education

Prevention is a cornerstone of our philosophy. We strongly emphasize preventative cardiology and vascular care. Regular check-ups, especially for individuals with risk factors such as high blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes, smoking history, or a family history of heart disease, are crucial for early detection and intervention.

Our team takes time to educate patients about heart health, risk factors, and lifestyle changes that can significantly reduce the risk of cardiovascular events. We believe an informed patient is a healthier patient, and this knowledge empowers individuals to take proactive steps towards maintaining a healthy heart and vascular system right here in Allen.
