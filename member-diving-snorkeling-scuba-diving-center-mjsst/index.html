Member Diving - Snorkeling & Scuba Diving Center

Discover the underwater wonders of Thailand's stunning coastline from our base in Bophut, Koh Samui. Member Diving is a Swiss diving school dedicated to providing exceptional diving experiences in the crystal-clear waters surrounding this beautiful island.

Our commitment to Swiss diving standards ensures that you receive top-notch training and guidance from certified instructors. Whether you're a complete beginner eager to explore the depths or an experienced diver seeking thrilling adventures, our team is here to help you make the most of your diving journey in Koh Samui.

We offer a comprehensive range of services designed for divers of all levels:

Beginner Diving Courses: Our PADI Open Water Diver course is the perfect starting point, equipping you with the skills and confidence to dive independently. Advanced Diving Adventures: From deeper dives to thrilling drift snorkeling, we provide guided excursions tailored to your experience level. Equipment Rental: Dive comfortably with our well-maintained rental gear, including tanks, regulators, and wetsuits. Snorkeling Tours: Explore vibrant coral reefs and encounter diverse marine life on our guided snorkeling trips departing from our Bophut location.

All our diving activities are conducted with safety as our top priority. We adhere strictly to international safety protocols and maintain the highest standards of equipment maintenance.

Located at 158/6 Moo 1, Bophut, our center is perfectly positioned to take you to some of the most beautiful dive sites in the area.

Ready to explore the underwater world? Visit our website https://member-diving.com/en/ or call us at +66 80 149 0444 to book your dive adventure today!

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you offer diving courses for complete beginners?

A: Yes, absolutely! We provide comprehensive PADI Open Water Diver courses designed specifically for beginners, teaching you everything you need to know to dive safely and confidently.

Q: What is the minimum age for diving with Member Diving?

A: Our introductory courses are suitable for individuals aged 10 and above. Junior Open Water Diver courses are available for those aged 10 to 15, with specific requirements.

Q: Do I need to book in advance for a dive trip?

A: It is highly recommended to book your dive trip in advance, especially during peak season. You can reserve your spot by calling us at +66 80 149 0444 or booking online.

Q: What dive sites are accessible from your Bophut location?

A: From our base in Bophut, we can take you to explore famous sites like Coral Bay, Big Buddha, and various other underwater locations known for their rich marine biodiversity.

Q: Is equipment rental included in the course price?

A: Yes, all necessary diving equipment is included in our course packages. We provide rental gear for both courses and individual dives.
