Thriller Miami Speedboat Adventures

Thriller Miami Speedboats offers the ultimate sightseeing adventure and is the only speedboat tour in Miami that takes you offshore along the world-famous South Beach (weather permitting). In just 45 thrilling minutes, you’ll experience Miami like never before—cruising past multi-million-dollar waterfront mansions on Star Island and Hibiscus Island, gliding by PortMiami and Fisher Island, and taking in stunning views of South Beach, Miami Beach, and the iconic Miami skyline. It’s a one-of-a-kind way to see the Magic City from the water, combining speed, excitement, and sightseeing all in one unforgettable ride. This family-friendly attraction is perfect for locals and visitors alike. Kids and adults both love the rush of the speedboat, the ocean breeze, and the unbeatable views of Miami’s famous landmarks. Tours run daily, with the first adventure departing at 10:00 AM and the last departure depending on the season and sunset. During the winter, the final tour leaves at 5:00 PM, while in the fall and spring, departures range between 6:00 PM and 7:00 PM. In the summer, you can even catch the last ride as late as 7:45 PM, making it a fantastic way to end the day with a breathtaking sunset over the water. Guests are asked to arrive at the dock at least 30 minutes prior to departure for check-in and boarding to ensure a smooth experience. For up-to-date tour times and availability, please visit our website before planning your trip. Whether you’re visiting Miami for the first time or a longtime resident looking for a fun new perspective of the city, Thriller Miami Speedboats is a must-do attraction. Don’t miss your chance to combine sightseeing, adventure, and the thrill of speed into one unforgettable Miami experience!
