Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):

Q1: What exactly does a brake inspection involve?

A brake inspection at Gowers Brake And Alignment in Raleigh involves a thorough check of all components of your braking system. This includes examining brake pads for wear, checking brake rotors for scoring or warping, inspecting brake calipers for proper function, testing brake lines and hoses for leaks, and ensuring the master cylinder is working correctly. We also check the brake fluid level and condition.

Q2: How often should I get an alignment service for my vehicle?

The recommended interval for wheel alignments can vary based on your vehicle's make and model, driving habits, and road conditions. Generally, it's a good idea to get an alignment service every 6,000 miles or every 6 months, whichever comes first. However, you should also get an alignment if you notice uneven tire wear, your vehicle pulls to one side while driving, or after hitting a pothole or curb.

Q3: What are the signs that my brakes need repair?

Several signs indicate your brakes may need attention. These include squealing or grinding noises when braking, a longer stopping distance than usual, the brake pedal feeling spongy or vibrating, a warning light on the dashboard (like the brake warning light), or a pulling sensation to one side when braking. Prompt attention is crucial for safety.

Q4: Can you explain the difference between brake pad replacement and rotor resurfacing?

Brake pads are the components that press against the rotors to create friction and slow the vehicle. When brake pads wear thin, they need to be replaced. Rotors are the large, flat discs that the brake pads clamp onto. If they become grooved, warped, or excessively worn, they may need to be resurfaced (machined smooth) or replaced. Resurfacing is often a cost-effective solution if the rotors are still in good structural condition.

Q5: Is it necessary to get a brake inspection even if my brakes seem fine?

A regular brake inspection is highly recommended, even if your brakes feel fine. Many brake issues develop gradually without obvious symptoms until a problem occurs. Routine inspections allow our technicians at Gowers Brake And Alignment to catch potential problems early, saving you money and ensuring your safety on the roads of Raleigh.
