Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) for Chubby Cheeks Pittsburgh

Q1: What is the difference between 3D and 4D ultrasound?

A1: Both 3D and 4D ultrasounds use advanced technology to create images beyond standard 2D scans. 3D provides a three-dimensional view, showing detailed facial features and body structures clearly. 4D builds on this by adding motion, allowing you to see your baby moving in real-time.

Q2: Is 3D/4D ultrasound safe for my baby?

A2: Yes. The 3D/4D ultrasound technology uses sound waves (similar to standard 2D ultrasound) and has been used safely for decades. We strictly adhere to the guidelines set by the FDA and the American Institute of Ultrasound in Medicine (AIUM) for safe imaging practices.

Q3: How early can I get a 4D ultrasound in Pittsburgh?

A3: A 4D ultrasound session typically requires a full bladder for optimal visualization. We recommend scheduling your appointment between weeks 14 and 16 of pregnancy for the best chance of seeing your baby clearly in 4D at our Pittsburgh location.

Q4: Do I need a prescription for a 3D/4D ultrasound in Pittsburgh?

A4: While a prescription is not legally required for elective 3D/4D ultrasounds, we often request a note from your obstetrician or midwife confirming your pregnancy. This helps ensure your pregnancy is progressing normally and allows us to coordinate with your healthcare provider if needed.

Q5: How long does a 3D/4D ultrasound appointment take in Pittsburgh?

A5: A standard 3D/4D ultrasound session at our Pittsburgh studio typically lasts between 30 and 45 minutes. This includes preparation time and the actual scanning process. We recommend allowing up to an hour for your visit.
