Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How soon should I contact a Fort Lauderdale car accident lawyer after my accident?

It's crucial to contact a Fort Lauderdale car accident lawyer as soon as possible after your accident. Florida has statutes of limitations that restrict the time you have to file a claim. Prompt legal contact allows us to investigate thoroughly, gather evidence while it's often fresh, consult with insurance companies appropriately, and build a strong case on your behalf to protect your rights and maximize your potential recovery.

Q: What types of compensation can I seek from a Fort Lauderdale car accident injury lawyer?

As a Fort Lauderdale client, you may be entitled to seek compensation for various damages resulting from your accident. This includes medical bills (past and future), lost wages (past and future), pain and suffering, emotional distress, property damage (like your vehicle), and potentially other related losses. Your Fort Lauderdale attorney will evaluate your specific situation and pursue all legally recoverable damages.

Q: Do I have to pay legal fees if my Fort Lauderdale car accident case doesn't settle?

Typically, Fort Lauderdale car accident cases handled by our firm utilize a contingency fee arrangement. This means you won't owe us any legal fees unless we successfully secure a settlement or verdict on your behalf. Our fee is contingent upon the outcome, making our legal services accessible and reducing your financial burden throughout the legal process from our Fort Lauderdale office.

Q: How long does a typical Fort Lauderdale car accident case take?

The duration of a Fort Lauderdale car accident case varies significantly based on factors like the severity of injuries, insurance company cooperation, the complexity of liability issues, and whether the case settles or goes to trial. Simple cases with clear liability and quick recovery might settle in a few months. More complex cases involving severe injuries, disputes over fault, or insurance challenges can take a year or longer. Your Fort Lauderdale attorney will provide a more specific timeline after evaluating your case details.

Q: Will the Fort Lauderdale car accident insurance company offer me a quick settlement?

Insurance companies in Fort Lauderdale, like many across the state, aim to settle cases quickly and for the lowest possible amount to minimize their costs. While they may make an initial offer, it's often designed to be low and discouraging. Our Fort Lauderdale car accident lawyers are experienced in negotiating with these insurance companies. We carefully analyze the full extent of your damages, including future medical needs and pain, and will fiercely advocate for a settlement that fairly compensates you for your losses, rather than accepting an inadequate quick settlement.
