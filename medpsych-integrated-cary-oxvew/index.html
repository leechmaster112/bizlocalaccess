Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What is TMS therapy?

A: Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (TMS) is a non-invasive procedure that uses magnetic fields to stimulate nerve cells in the brain to improve symptoms of depression and other mental health conditions. At MedPsych Integrated - Cary, we utilize TMS as a treatment option for eligible patients.

Q2: Is TMS therapy safe?

A: TMS is generally considered a safe treatment option. At our Cary location, our team closely monitors patients during treatment to ensure their safety and comfort throughout the process.

Q3: How does TMS treatment work?

A: TMS uses an electromagnetic coil placed near the scalp to deliver targeted magnetic pulses. These pulses gently stimulate specific areas of the brain involved in mood regulation. Our Cary-based practitioners explain the process thoroughly before beginning treatment.

Q4: Who is a good candidate for TMS at MedPsych Integrated - Cary?

A: TMS is typically recommended for individuals experiencing treatment-resistant depression. Our Cary mental health professionals conduct thorough evaluations to determine if TMS is appropriate for each patient's specific situation.
