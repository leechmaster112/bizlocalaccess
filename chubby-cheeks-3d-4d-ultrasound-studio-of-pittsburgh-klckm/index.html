Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What kind of ultrasound services do you offer at your Pittsburgh location?

We specialize in providing elective 3D and 4D ultrasounds for expectant parents in the Pittsburgh area. These advanced imaging techniques offer detailed views of the developing baby.

Q2: Is 3D/4D ultrasound safe for my baby?

Yes, the 3D and 4D ultrasound technology we use at our Pittsburgh studio is considered safe and non-invasive. We follow the guidelines set by the FDA and medical associations for diagnostic ultrasound.

Q3: How early can I get a 3D/4D ultrasound at your Pittsburgh studio?

We typically recommend scheduling your 3D/4D ultrasound appointment between 14 and 36 weeks of pregnancy for the clearest images. Sometimes, clearer images can be obtained slightly earlier.

Q4: Do you offer gender determination with your 3D/4D ultrasounds?

Yes, gender determination is often possible with our 3D/4D ultrasound services, depending on the baby's position and development stage.

Q5: Do I need a prescription for a 3D/4D ultrasound at your Pittsburgh location?

No prescription is required for our elective 3D/4D ultrasound services offered at our Pittsburgh studio. These are considered diagnostic imaging services provided for informational and bonding purposes.
