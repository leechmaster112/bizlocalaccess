Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How long does an epoxy floor installation typically take?

A1: The duration of an epoxy floor project at our 241 Bascom Ave location depends on the size of the area, the complexity of the design, and the specific epoxy system used. Generally, the actual installation process takes 1-3 days. However, the full curing time for the epoxy to reach its maximum strength and durability is typically 5-7 days, during which the floor should not be fully trafficked.

Q2: Can epoxy flooring be used in commercial settings like restaurants or retail stores?

A2: Absolutely! Epoxy flooring is an excellent choice for commercial spaces in Pittsburgh. It offers superior durability, chemical resistance, and hygienic properties, making it ideal for high-traffic areas like restaurants, retail stores, showrooms, and offices located right here in our community.

Q3: Is epoxy flooring suitable for residential garages?

A3: Yes, epoxy flooring is very popular for residential garages in Pittsburgh. It provides a durable, easy-to-clean surface that can withstand vehicle traffic and spills. Many homeowners choose epoxy for its aesthetic appeal, offering a professional finish that can even hide cracks and imperfections in the concrete.

Q4: How do I maintain an epoxy floor?

A4: Maintaining an epoxy floor is relatively simple. Regular sweeping or dry mopping is usually sufficient. For spills, prompt cleaning is recommended. Avoid using harsh, abrasive cleaners or tools that could potentially damage the surface. Our team at ArmorX Pittsburgh Epoxy Floors can provide specific care instructions based on the epoxy system used for your project.

Q5: Can you match existing floor colors or create custom designs?

A5: Yes, we can often match existing colors or shades. For custom designs, we offer a wide range of color combinations, metallic flakes, and decorative patterns. During your consultation at our 241 Bascom Ave location, we'll work with you to create a unique and beautiful floor design that suits your style and functional needs.
