2. Mighty Networks

Mighty Networks is a popular choice for creators and entrepreneurs. It combines community features with course creation capabilities, allowing users to monetize their knowledge and build a community around their content.

Pros

  • Integrated monetization options.
  • User-friendly interface.
  • Mobile app compatibility

Cons

  • Fewer advanced customization options.
  • Limited analytics compared to some of its competitors.

Who It's Best For

Creators, coaches, and educators who want to monetize their expertise and build a community around their courses.

3. Circle

Circle is a platform focused on community building with a clean and organized layout. It emphasizes conversations, with features like forums, live streams, and direct messaging to facilitate engagement.

Pros

  • Clean and simple.
  • Focus on facilitating conversations.
  • Integration with other tools.

Cons

  • Limited customization.
  • Can be pricier as you scale.

Who It's Best For

Businesses and individuals needing a clean, straightforward platform to encourage member interaction and discussion.

4. Discord

Initially designed for gamers, Discord has evolved into a versatile platform for building communities of all types. It offers features like voice and video calls, text channels, and bot integrations.

Pros

  • Highly flexible.
  • Free version available with many features.
  • Strong real-time communication capabilities.

Cons

  • Difficult to manage large communities without moderation.
  • Can feel disorganized without meticulous channel setup.

Who It's Best For

Gaming communities, hobby groups, and those wanting a free or low-cost solution with real-time communication functionality.

5. Tribe

Tribe focuses on community-driven content. It is a more modern platform that has social networking features, and tools facilitating user generated content.

Pros

  • Emphasis on user-generated content.
  • Easy to set up and manage.
  • Good for brand awareness and discussions.

Cons

  • Limited customization.
  • Some features not available on certain plans.

Who It's Best For

Businesses that are looking to host customer and fan communities, or those focused on social networking functionality.

6. Facebook Groups

Facebook Groups are a widely accessible option for community building, leveraging the existing Facebook infrastructure and user base. It offers different group types and engagement tools.

Pros

  • Large potential audience reach.
  • Easy to set up.
  • Familiar interface for most users.

Cons

  • Limited control over the user experience and branding.
  • Heavy competition.
  • Algorithms can affect content visibility.

Who It's Best For

Those wanting to leverage the existing Facebook user base and reach, at little-to-no cost.

7. Discourse

Discourse is an open-source forum platform known for its modern and intuitive design. It focuses on well-organized discussions and offers excellent SEO-friendliness.

Pros

  • Open-source and customizable.
  • Excellent for SEO.
  • Well-organized discussion forums.

Cons

  • Technical expertise needed for self-hosting.
  • Can be complex for beginners.

Who It's Best For

Organizations and businesses seeking a highly customizable forum with a focus on SEO and in-depth discussions that have some technical expertise.

FAQ

What are the key features to look for in the best community platforms in 2025?

Key features include robust moderation, flexible customization options, strong analytics, engaging content tools, and ease of use for both administrators and members.

How important is customization to a community platform?

Customization is critical for aligning your community platform with your brand identity and providing a unique and personalized experience for your audience.

What are good platforms to create online communities in 2025?

As covered in this guide, the best community platforms in 2025 include BTS, Mighty Networks, Circle, Discord, Tribe, Facebook Groups, and Discourse.

Which platform is best for monetization in an online community?

Mighty Networks and BTS both offer strong monetization features, allowing you to charge for access to content, courses, or events.

How can a community ensure high engagement?

High engagement results from many elements: regular content, active moderation, events, promotions, and enabling easy interaction among members.

Is it possible to migrate from one platform to another?

Yes, migration is often possible, but dependent on the platform features. Some platforms provide tools to transfer content and members. Plan for this up-front.

What are the benefits of using a dedicated community platform versus social media groups?

Dedicated platforms give more control over branding, content delivery, data/analytics, and member experience, whereas social media groups can be impacted by outside algorithms.

How important is mobile optimization in 2025 community platforms?

Mobile optimization is critical, given that most users access online platforms via their smartphones. Platforms should offer responsive designs and/or mobile apps.

How do I choose the right platform for my community in 2025?

Evaluate your needs, target audience, budget, and desired features. Test different platforms to see which best fits your goals. BTS offers a powerful range of tools.

What should I know about data privacy with my community platform?

Ensure the platform complies with data privacy regulations (like GDPR and CCPA) and that you are transparent with your community about data handling, along with offering strong security features.
