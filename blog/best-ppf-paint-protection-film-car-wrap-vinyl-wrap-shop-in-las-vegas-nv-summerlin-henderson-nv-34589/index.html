FAQ: Your Questions About PPF, Car Wraps, and Vinyl Wraps Answered (2026)

1. What is PPF (Paint Protection Film), and how does it protect my car?

PPF, or Paint Protection Film, is a clear, self-healing urethane film applied to your car's painted surfaces. It acts as a shield against scratches, rock chips, UV damage, and other environmental hazards, preserving your car's paint and value in 2026.

2. How long does PPF last?

High-quality PPF, when professionally installed, can last from 5 to 10 years, or even longer, depending on the film type and environmental conditions. Dipped Auto Works offers top-tier PPF known for its durability in 2026.

Car wraps offer several benefits, including customization options with various colors and designs, protection of the original paint from minor scratches and UV rays, and the ability to easily change the appearance of your car without a permanent paint job in 2026.
