How can I find a list of Tesla Approved Body Shops in Los Angeles?

The most reliable way to find the current list is to check Tesla's official website, which typically has a locator tool for approved collision centers. It's always best to cross-reference this with direct contact to the shop.

Can any body shop repair a Tesla, or do they need special features?

Teslas have unique construction, battery systems, and advanced electronics. Approved shops need specialized tools for working with high-voltage systems, advanced alignment equipment for lightweight materials, and technicians trained specifically on Tesla models and repair procedures.

What if my insurance company suggests a non-approved body shop?

You have the right to choose your repair facility. While your insurance company may recommend a shop, you can insist on taking your Tesla to a Tesla Approved Body Shop. Discuss this with your insurance adjuster to ensure coverage aligns with repairs at an approved facility.

How long does Tesla approval last for a body shop?

Tesla approval is not permanent; it requires periodic renewal. Shops must maintain their standards, continue technician training, and ensure their equipment remains up-to-date to keep their approved status. Always verify the shop's current approval status.

Are Tesla Approved Body Shops more expensive?

While specialized repair can sometimes come at a premium due to the expertise, equipment, and genuine parts involved, the cost is often justified by the quality, safety, and warranty preservation. It's essential to get detailed estimates and compare value, not just price.

What specific Tesla models can an approved body shop repair?

How do I check if a specific Los Angeles body shop is indeed Tesla Approved?
