Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What should I do immediately after a car accident in Calgary?

A: Ensure everyone's safety, exchange information with other drivers, gather witness contact details, and report the accident to the police depending upon the severity. Seek immediate medical attention even if you feel fine, as some injuries may not be immediately apparent. Then, contact a car accident lawyer as soon as possible.

Q: How much does it cost to hire a car accident lawyer in Calgary?

A: Most car accident lawyers in Calgary work on a contingency fee basis, often referred to as 'no win, no fee.' This means you only pay lawyers' fees if they successfully recover compensation for you. At Preszler Injury Lawyers, for instance, initial consultations are free.

Q: How long do I have to file a claim for a car accident in Alberta?

A: The statute of limitations in Alberta for personal injury claims, including car accidents, is generally two years from the date of the accident. It's crucial to consult with a lawyer as soon as possible to ensure your claim is filed within this timeframe.

Q: What kind of compensation can I get after a car accident in Calgary?

A: You may be entitled to compensation for various damages, including medical expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, and other accident-related financial losses. The specific types and amounts of compensation depend on the specifics of each case.

Q: How do car accident lawyers prove fault in a Calgary car accident case?

A: Lawyers use evidence such as police reports, witness statements, accident reconstruction analysis, and photographs to determine the at-fault driver. They then use this evidence to build a case supporting your right to compensation.

Q: What if the other driver was uninsured in a Calgary car accident?

A: If the other driver is uninsured, you can often make a claim under your own automobile insurance policy for uninsured motorist coverage. A skilled car accident lawyer can explain the specifics of this coverage and help you file the appropriate claim.

Q: How long does a car accident case usually take in Calgary?

A: The length of a car accident case can vary significantly, depending on the complexity of the accident, the extent of injuries, and the willingness of the parties to settle. Some cases may resolve within months, while others may take a year or longer, especially if they proceed to trial.

Q: Should I speak with the insurance company after a car accident without a lawyer in Calgary?

A: It's generally advisable to consult with a car accident lawyer before speaking with the insurance company. Insurance adjusters are trained to minimize payouts; a lawyer can protect your rights and help you navigate communications with the insurance company effectively.

Q: What are the benefits of hiring a car accident lawyer in Calgary?

A: A car accident lawyer can help you: understand your rights, investigate the accident, gather evidence, negotiate with insurance companies, and represent you in court if necessary. They can also provide peace of mind and help you maximize your compensation.

Q: Where can I find the best car accident lawyers in Calgary?

A: Online directories, referrals from friends or family, and legal review websites are good places to start. Look for a lawyer specializing in personal injury law with extensive experience in car accident cases, such as Preszler Injury Lawyers.
