Q3: How do I choose the best Annapolis accident attorney for my situation?

Q4: What if I was partially at fault for the accident in Annapolis?

Maryland follows a rule of "contributory negligence." This means that if you are found to be even 1% at fault for an accident, you may not be able to recover damages. This is why having a skilled attorney who can argue against your fault or minimize it is crucial.

Q5: How long do I have to file an accident claim in Annapolis?

Maryland has a statute of limitations for personal injury claims, which is generally three years from the date of the accident. However, it's always best to consult with an attorney as soon as possible to ensure timely filing and preserve your rights.

Q6: What kind of compensation can I expect from an Annapolis accident attorney?

Compensation can include medical expenses (past and future), lost wages and earning capacity, property damage, pain and suffering, emotional distress, and other related economic and non-economic losses. An experienced attorney will help you identify and pursue all eligible damages.

Q7: Should I talk to the insurance company before hiring an Annapolis accident attorney?

It's generally advisable not to give recorded statements or detailed accounts to the at-fault party's insurance company without consulting an attorney first. They may try to use your words against you. An attorney can handle all communications to protect your interests.
