FAQ

Here are some frequently asked questions about choosing a wood-burning stove company in the South West:

What should I look for when choosing a wood-burning stove company?

  • Experience and Reputation: Look for established companies with positive customer reviews and a good track record.
  • Qualified Installers: Ensure the company employs HETAS-registered installers.
  • Wide Selection: A company with a diverse range of stoves from various brands allows you to find the perfect match for your needs.
  • After-Sales Support: Choose a company that offers reliable after-sales service and ongoing support.

How important is it to use a HETAS-registered installer?

It's crucial to use a HETAS-registered installer. They are trained and certified to ensure the stove is installed safely and complies with building regulations. This also usually affects your home insurance.

What types of stoves are available?

There are various types of wood-burning stoves, including freestanding stoves, inset stoves, and boiler stoves. The best type for you will depend on your heating needs, space constraints, and design preferences.

How do I know what size stove I need?

An experienced stove company will conduct a site survey to assess the size of your room, the level of insulation, and other factors to determine the appropriate stove size and heat output. They will need accurate measurements of the room and an understanding of the building's thermal properties.

What are the benefits of a wood-burning stove?

Wood-burning stoves provide efficient and cost-effective heating. They offer a cozy ambiance, reduce reliance on fossil fuels, and can increase the value of your home. They can be particularly useful in rural locations.

What is the typical lifespan of a wood-burning stove?

With proper maintenance and regular servicing, a wood-burning stove can last for 15-20 years or even longer, depending on the quality of the stove and how frequently it's used.

What are the regulations surrounding wood-burning stoves in the South West?

Wood-burning stove installations must comply with building regulations to ensure safety and efficiency. This includes proper flue installation, air supply, and the use of approved fuels. Your chosen company should guide you through all regulations. Be sure you are burning the right type of wood too.

Where can I buy seasoned firewood in the South West?

Most reputable stove companies also supply seasoned firewood. You can find suppliers online or ask your installer for recommendations. Using properly seasoned wood is essential for efficient burning and reducing emissions.

Can I install a wood-burning stove myself?

While it is technically possible to self-install a wood-burning stove, it is strongly recommended that you use a qualified, HETAS-registered installer. Incorrect installation can be dangerous and may void your home insurance. In certain areas, it is illegal for unqualified people to work on such projects.

How do I maintain my wood-burning stove?

Regular maintenance includes cleaning the chimney, removing ash, checking seals, and inspecting the stove's components. Your installer can advise you on the specific maintenance requirements for your stove.

What are the different types of stove designs available?

There are a huge varieties. Traditionally there are stoves that sit in a fireplace opening and freestanding stoves (usually sitting on a hearth). Stoves are made in cast iron, steel, and a variety of other materials. Various glass designs, and finishes.
