Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Here are some frequently asked questions about cleaning services in San Diego:

  1. What types of cleaning services are available in San Diego? San Diego cleaning services offer a wide range, including regular house cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, commercial cleaning, and specialized services like vacation rental cleaning. Many also offer add-on services like window cleaning and oven cleaning.
  2. How much does it cost to hire a cleaning service in San Diego? Prices vary depending on the size of your home, the services required, and the frequency of cleaning. Generally, you can expect to pay anywhere from $100 to upwards of $400 for a typical cleaning, with deep cleans and specialized services costing more. Getting multiple quotes is advisable.
  3. How do I choose the best cleaning service for my needs? Consider factors like the services offered, pricing, customer reviews, their reputation, and their service area. Make sure they offer the specific services you need, like green cleaning options if that's important to you. Read reviews to gauge their quality of service.
  4. What areas of San Diego do cleaning services cover? Most cleaning services in San Diego serve a broad area, but it’s best to verify their service area before booking. Many cover the downtown area, coastal communities, and suburbs, but some may have limitations. Check their website or call to confirm.
  5. Are cleaning services in San Diego insured and bonded? Reputable cleaning services in San Diego are typically insured and bonded, protecting you from liability in case of accidents or damages. Always ask about this and verify their credentials before hiring.
  6. What should I do to prepare for a cleaning service? Before your cleaning service arrives, pick up clutter, put away any fragile or valuable items, and provide clear instructions regarding any specific needs or preferences. If you have pets, make sure they are safely contained.
  7. How often should I have my house cleaned? The frequency of your cleaning service depends on your lifestyle and needs. Many people choose weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly cleaning. Deep cleaning services are often done less frequently, such as quarterly or seasonally.
  8. Do cleaning services in San Diego offer eco-friendly or green cleaning options? Yes, many San Diego cleaning services offer eco-friendly cleaning options. When requesting a quote, ask about their use of eco-friendly cleaning products and practices if this is a priority for you.
  9. Can I customize my cleaning service with a cleaning company? Yes, most cleaning services in San Diego allow you to customize your service. You can typically request specific tasks you want the cleaners to focus on or provide special instructions for certain areas of your home. Always communicate your needs during the booking process.
  10. What is included in a deep cleaning service? A deep cleaning service goes beyond regular cleaning by including tasks like cleaning inside the oven, refrigerator, and microwave, cleaning window sills and blinds, and often washing baseboards, and other detailed cleaning tasks that are not typically done during regular cleanings.
  11. What happens if I'm not satisfied with the cleaning service? Reputable cleaning services typically offer a satisfaction guarantee. If you are not happy with the service, you should contact the company immediately. Many will return to re-clean the areas in question, or offer a discount on future services.
  12. How do I book a cleaning service in San Diego? Most cleaning services offer easy online booking or you can schedule your cleaning by phone. Be sure to provide the details of your home and ask any clarifying questions you may have before confirming.
