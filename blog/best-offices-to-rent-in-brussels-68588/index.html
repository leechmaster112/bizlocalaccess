Frequently Asked Questions About Renting Offices in Brussels (2026)

What are the most affordable offices to rent in Brussels?

Affordability in Brussels office rentals can depend on location, size, and included amenities. Coworking spaces often offer more budget-friendly options for individuals and small teams, while serviced offices in prime districts might have higher price points. It's advisable to compare pricing models and consider the total value proposition.

Where can I find flexible office leases in Brussels?

Many providers in Brussels offer flexible lease options, including month-to-month agreements, 6-month commitments, and longer-term contracts. Coworking spaces and serviced office providers are generally the most flexible in this regard.

What is the average cost of a private office in Brussels?

The average cost for a private office in Brussels can range widely, from approximately €400 to €1200+ per month, depending on the size, location (e.g., EU Quarter, City Centre, Louise district), and amenities offered by the business center. It's crucial to get specific quotes.

Are there coworking spaces with private offices available in Brussels?

Yes, many coworking spaces in Brussels, such as Louise 200, Betacowork, and Spaces, offer dedicated private offices in addition to shared workspaces. This allows businesses to benefit from the coworking community while maintaining their own private space.

What are the best business districts for office rentals in Brussels?

Key business districts include the European Quarter (for EU-related institutions), the Central Business District (CBD) around Central Station, the prestigious Louise district, and the North Quarter (partially). Each offers different advantages in terms of accessibility, prestige, and amenities.

How can I find serviced offices in Brussels?

Serviced offices in Brussels can be found through dedicated business center operators like AAB Business Center, Louise 200, and others. Websites specializing in commercial real estate and flexible office solutions are also excellent resources.

What amenities should I look for in an office space to rent in Brussels?

Essential amenities often include high-speed internet, fully equipped meeting rooms, professional reception services, printing facilities, kitchen areas, and secure access. The importance of each will depend on your business's specific needs.

Is it possible to rent a virtual office in Brussels?

Yes, many business centers and coworking spaces in Brussels offer virtual office services. These typically include a prestigious business address, mail handling, and phone answering services, without the need for a physical workspace.

Which offices in Brussels are best for startups?

Startups often benefit from coworking spaces that offer a strong community, networking opportunities, and flexible plans. Betacowork and Louise 200 are excellent choices, providing environments conducive to growth and collaboration.

What are the benefits of renting an office in the Louise district?

Renting an office in the Louise district, as offered by Louise 200, provides a highly prestigious address, excellent accessibility, a sophisticated atmosphere, and proximity to high-end amenities and businesses, offering a distinguished image for your company.
