What is general liability insurance for contractors?

General liability insurance for contractors provides coverage for property damage or bodily injury claims. It helps protect the business from financial loss resulting from accidents or incidents that occur on a job site or at a client's property.

What is professional liability insurance and why do contractors need it?

Professional liability insurance, also known as errors and omissions insurance, protects contractors against claims of negligence, errors, or omissions in their professional services. This type of insurance is especially important for contractors who provide design or consulting services and offer warranty coverage.

How can I save money on contractor insurance in 2026?

In 2026, you can save money on contractor insurance by comparing quotes from multiple providers. What is workers' compensation insurance and why do contractors need it?

Workers' compensation insurance protects employers and employees in case of workplace injuries or illnesses. It covers medical expenses, lost wages, and rehabilitation costs for employees injured on the job. Contracting companies are generally legally required to carry workers' compensation insurance for their employees.
