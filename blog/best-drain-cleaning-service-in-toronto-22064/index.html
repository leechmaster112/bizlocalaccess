Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the most common cause of clogged drains in Toronto?

The most common causes of clogged drains in Toronto are often a combination of food waste, grease, hair, soap scum, and the accumulation of foreign objects. Toronto's aging infrastructure can also contribute with pipe corrosion and issues with tree roots.

How can I prevent drain clogs in my home?

You can prevent drain clogs by using drain strainers, avoiding pouring grease down the drain, and regularly flushing your drains with hot water. Consider having your drains professionally inspected and cleaned regularly to prevent buildup.

When should I call a professional drain cleaning service?

You should call a professional drain cleaning service when you experience persistent slow drains, backed-up toilets, gurgling sounds in your pipes, or unpleasant odors. If these home remedies do not work, it is time to call a professional.

What methods do professional drain cleaners use?

Professional drain cleaners use a variety of methods, including drain snakes (augers) to break up clogs, hydro-jetting to blast away debris with high-pressure water, and video inspection to identify the cause of the problem.

How much does drain cleaning typically cost in Toronto?

The cost of drain cleaning in Toronto varies depending on the severity of the clog, the method used, and the plumbing company. Prices can range from around $100 to $500 or more for complex problems.

Do you offer emergency drain cleaning services?

Some of the services on this list, such as Tornado Plumbing & Drains, offer 24/7 emergency drain cleaning services, ensuring you get help when you need it most. Check with your chosen service provider for their specific availability.

How do I choose the right drain cleaning service in Toronto?

When selecting a drain cleaning service, consider factors such as their experience, reviews, pricing, and availability. Be sure they are fully licensed, insured, and offer transparent pricing. Also make sure they have good references from people in Toronto, such as neighbors.

Can tree roots cause drain problems?

Yes, tree roots can be a significant cause of drain problems, especially in older homes with older pipes. Tree roots can penetrate pipe joints, causing clogs and damage. Professional inspections with video cameras can help locate these issues.

Are there any DIY drain cleaning methods that work?

Yes, there are some DIY methods that can work for minor clogs, such as using a plunger, pouring hot water down the drain, or using a mixture of baking soda and vinegar. Be cautious of harsh chemicals, which can damage your pipes.

How often should I have my drains cleaned professionally?

The frequency of professional drain cleaning depends on various factors, including the age and condition of your pipes, your usage habits, and whether you have a history of clogs. Generally, it's a good idea to have your drains inspected and cleaned every one to two years, but some may need it more frequently.
