FAQ

Q: What services do private investigators in Sydney offer?

A: Sydney private investigators offer a wide range of services, including surveillance, background checks, infidelity investigations, missing persons searches, asset tracing, corporate investigations (fraud, embezzlement), and more.

Q: How much does it cost to hire a private investigator in Sydney?

A: The cost varies depending on the complexity of the case, the services required, and the investigator's experience and expertise. Costs may include hourly rates, retainers, and expenses such as travel and surveillance equipment. It's best to get a quote and discuss the scope of work beforehand.

Q: How do I choose the best private investigator for my needs in Sydney?

A: Consider your specific needs, the investigator's experience, their specialization (e.g., infidelity, corporate), their reputation and reviews, their licensing and credentials, and their communication style. Always conduct due diligence before hiring.

Q: Are private investigators in Sydney licensed?

A: Yes, private investigators in Australia, including Sydney, must be licensed to operate. Ensure the firm you choose holds the appropriate licenses and complies with all state and federal regulations.

Q: How long does an investigation usually take?

A: The duration of an investigation depends on the complexity of the case. Some investigations might be completed in a few days, while others could take several weeks or months. The investigator will provide an estimated timeline.

Q: What kind of information can a private investigator obtain?

A: Licensed private investigators can gather information through legal means, including surveillance, public record searches, interviews, and database searches. They cannot engage in illegal activities like wiretapping or hacking.

Q: How do private investigators maintain the privacy of their clients?

A: Reputable private investigators prioritize client confidentiality. They will keep all information obtained during the investigation strictly confidential and comply with all privacy regulations. They will not disclose any information to third parties without permission.

Q: What types of cases are private investigators in Sydney most known for handling?

A: Private investigators in Sydney are often hired for infidelity investigations, child custody matters, missing persons cases, and corporate fraud investigations. These are common needs within the city and surrounding areas.

Q: Can a private investigator help me find someone in Sydney that I have lost contact with?

A: Yes, a licensed private investigator in Sydney can assist in locating missing persons, utilizing techniques and databases not available to the general public. They can often provide a solution to locating missing loved ones.

Q: What are the legal limitations on what a private investigator can do in Sydney?

A: Private investigators must operate within the law. They cannot engage in illegal activities such as wiretapping, hacking, or trespass. They cannot obtain information they are not permitted to, such as tax records.

Q: How do I report a bad private investigator in Sydney?

A: You can report a private investigator to the relevant licensing authority in your state or territory. You may also consult with a lawyer to consider other options.
