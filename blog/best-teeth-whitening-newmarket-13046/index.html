2. How much does teeth whitening cost in Newmarket?

The cost of teeth whitening in Newmarket varies depending on the type of treatment. 3. Are teeth whitening treatments safe?

Teeth whitening treatments, when performed by a dental professional, are generally safe. Dental professionals, like those at Dental Hygiene Care, take steps to minimize potential risks like tooth sensitivity or gum irritation. Before beginning, a dentist or hygienist will perform an oral health exam to ensure that teeth and gums are healthy and suitable for whitening. Remember to check with your dentist for more information.

4. How long do teeth whitening results last?

The longevity of teeth whitening results depends on the treatment and individual factors such as diet and habits. In-office treatments can last from several months to a few years. Maintaining good oral hygiene and avoiding stain-causing foods and drinks can help extend the results. Touch-up treatments may be needed over time.

5. Can I whiten my teeth if I have sensitive teeth?

Yes, but you should discuss this with your dentist. They can recommend treatments designed to reduce sensitivity or offer customized treatment plans. They may also suggest the application of desensitizing toothpaste a few weeks before whitening. Dental Hygiene Care's professionals can assess the extent of sensitivity and determine the most appropriate whitening options for your specific needs.

6. What causes teeth discoloration?

Teeth discoloration can result from various factors, including aging, genetics, diet, smoking, and certain medications. Consumption of staining foods and beverages like coffee, tea, red wine, and berries can contribute to discoloration. Avoiding these can prolong whitening effects.

7. What are the best methods for maintaining white teeth after whitening?

To maintain your results after teeth whitening, it's essential to follow these steps: maintain good oral hygiene by brushing and flossing regularly; avoid stain-causing foods and beverages; use a whitening toothpaste; and schedule regular dental check-ups. Consider professional touch-up treatments as recommended by your dentist.

8. Can teeth whitening damage my enamel?

Professional teeth whitening, when performed correctly, is unlikely to damage enamel. The products used in most methods often contain low concentrations of hydrogen peroxide or carbamide peroxide. However, overusing whitening products or improper use can cause sensitivity or, theoretically, damage. Always follow the dentist’s recommendations.

9. Which teeth whitening treatments are right for sensitive teeth?

If you have sensitive teeth, the best options include those delivered under professional supervision, where the procedure can be customized. Your dentist (such as at Dental Hygiene Care) can take precautions to manage the sensitivity, such as using lower concentrations of whitening agents or supplying desensitizing treatments. It is also important to discuss this at the time of your checkup.
