FAQ: Best Wedding Florist in Pittsburgh

1. How far in advance should I book my wedding florist in Pittsburgh?

It’s best to book your florist as early as possible, ideally 6-12 months before your wedding. For popular dates, especially in peak wedding season (May-October), it's wise to book even earlier, perhaps 12-18 months in advance, particularly for standout florists like Lilac & Fawn.

2. What information should I have ready when contacting a wedding florist?

Have your wedding date, venue, guest count, and any initial ideas about your desired style and color palette prepared. Inspiration photos and a rough budget range are also helpful.

3. How much do wedding flowers typically cost in Pittsburgh?

Wedding floral costs vary widely depending on factors such as flower types, design complexity, guest count, and venue size. It is best to reach out to different florists in Pittsburgh. A starting budget for your flowers is a great idea.

4. What types of floral arrangements do wedding florists offer?

Typical services include bridal bouquets, bridesmaid bouquets, boutonnieres, corsages, ceremony décor (altar flowers, arches), reception centerpieces, cake flowers, and flower installations.

5. How can I choose the best wedding florist for my style?

Review florists' portfolios, read reviews, and schedule consultations. Choose a florist whose style resonates with you and who understands your vision for your wedding.

6. What questions should I ask during a florist consultation?

Ask about their experience, design process, flower availability, pricing structure, and cancellation policies. Inquire if they can work with your budget and any logistical considerations.

7. Do florists offer delivery and setup services in Pittsburgh?

Most wedding florists in the Pittsburgh area, including Lilac & Fawn, offer delivery and setup services to ensure your flowers arrive fresh and are properly installed at your venues.

8. Can I provide specific flower inspiration to my florist?

Absolutely! Florists welcome inspiration photos, color palettes, and ideas to help them create arrangements that reflect your style. The more information you provide, the better they can understand and execute your vision.

9. How do I decide on a budget for my wedding flowers?

Consider your overall wedding budget and assign a realistic percentage to flowers. The cost of flowers you choose will depend on the arrangements, style, and the number of flowers required for your wedding. Be prepared to provide a price range and consider other aspects of your event to ensure the flowers match your overall vision.

10. What happens if my favorite flowers are out of season?

Your florist will work with you to find suitable alternatives that complement your color scheme and design. Seasonality is a factor when planning, but there are always beautiful options available.

11. Should I have flowers at my ceremony and reception?

The answer is often "yes"! Floral arrangements can add so much to the atmosphere of both spaces. Many couples opt to have ceremony and reception flowers, although this is dependent on budget and overall wedding scheme.
