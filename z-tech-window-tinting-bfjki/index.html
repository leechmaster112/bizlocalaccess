Frequently Asked Questions

What types of window tint do you offer at your Countryside shop?

We offer a selection of high-quality automotive window films, including ceramic, carbon, and hybrid tints. Each type provides different levels of heat rejection, UV protection, and glare reduction. Our technicians at 9825 W 55th St #D can help you choose the best film for your vehicle and needs.

How long does a window tint installation take at your location?

The time required depends on the vehicle, but most standard car tinting services are completed within 2 to 4 hours at our facility. We ask that you plan accordingly so we can give your car the time it needs for a perfect application.

Do I need an appointment for window tinting at Z Tech Window Tinting?

Yes, we highly recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure we have the time reserved to provide you with our full attention and service. You can schedule your appointment by calling us at 708-354-9555.

How do I care for my new window tint after installation?

We will provide you with detailed aftercare instructions before you leave our location. Generally, we advise not rolling down your windows and avoiding cleaning the interior film surface for several days to allow the tint to fully cure and adhere properly.
