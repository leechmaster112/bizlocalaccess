Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of foundation problems do you repair?

We address common foundation issues experienced by Rockwall homeowners, including cracks in walls or foundations, uneven settling leading to sloped floors, foundation bowing or movement, and structural instability caused by various factors.

Q2: How do I know if my foundation needs repair?

Signs that may indicate foundation problems include visible cracks in walls, floors, or foundations; doors and windows that stick or no longer close properly; gaps appearing between walls and floors or ceilings; and unusual tilting or leaning of the structure. A professional inspection is the best way to confirm.

Q3: How long does a typical foundation repair project take?

The duration of a foundation repair project depends on the complexity of the issue and the chosen repair method. Our team works efficiently to complete the necessary repairs, minimizing disruption to your home and schedule. We provide you with a clear timeline during our initial consultation.

Q4: What is the cost of foundation repair?

The cost varies based on the specific problem, its severity, the size of the foundation, and the required repair method. We offer a free, no-obligation foundation inspection and estimate at our Rockwall location to provide you with a detailed breakdown of potential costs.

Q5: Do I need a permit for foundation repair?

Often, foundation repair work requires permits from the city or county. Our team is familiar with the local regulations in Rockwall and will guide you through the permitting process if necessary, ensuring compliance with all requirements.
