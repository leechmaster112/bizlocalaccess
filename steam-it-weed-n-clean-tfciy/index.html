Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: How does steam kill weeds effectively?

A: Our specialized equipment emits high-heat, low-pressure steam, reaching temperatures hot enough to destroy plant tissue, including the root system, on contact. This thermal treatment prevents regrowth and eliminates weeds naturally.

Q2: Is steam weed control effective on tough Vancouver weeds like dandelions?

A: Yes, steam is highly effective against a wide range of common Vancouver weeds, including dandelions. Multiple precise applications are usually needed for complete eradication, especially for established roots.

Q3: How soon can you typically treat my Vancouver property after booking?

A: We aim for prompt service. Scheduling depends on the size of the property and the extent of the weed problem, but we strive to accommodate your needs within a reasonable timeframe after booking.

Q4: Is your steam cleaning safe for all surfaces in Vancouver?

A: Our low-pressure steam is generally safe for most hard, stable surfaces like concrete, wood decks, and paving stones. We will discuss the specific surfaces on your property to ensure steam cleaning is appropriate and effective.

Q5: Do you offer follow-up services to prevent weed regrowth in Vancouver?

A: Absolutely. We recommend a follow-up service, typically scheduled a few weeks after the initial treatment, to target any new weeds that may have germinated and to ensure lasting results for your Vancouver property.
