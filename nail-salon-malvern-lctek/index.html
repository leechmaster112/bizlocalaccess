Frequently Asked Questions

1. What services does Nail Salon Malvern offer?

We provide a full range of nail services including classic manicures, deluxe manicures with polish changes, pedicures, basic nail shaping, acrylic nail enhancements, soft gel overlays, nail art designs, and specialized treatments like nail repairs and cuticle care.

2. How long does a typical nail service take?

A standard manicure usually takes about 30-45 minutes, while a deluxe manicure with polish change may take 45-60 minutes. Pedicures generally require 45-60 minutes for basic care and 60-90 minutes for deluxe treatments. Complex nail art or extensions may take longer, typically 60-90 minutes per hand.

3. Do you file or shape the nails during a manicure?

Yes, a proper nail shape is essential for healthy nails and the longevity of polish and enhancements. Our technicians will shape your nails to your desired style (typically square, square oval, round, squoval, oval, or almond) as part of the manicure service.

4. Can I purchase your nail products?

We primarily use professional-grade products for in-salon services to ensure quality and durability. While we don't typically sell these specific products directly to clients, we can recommend brands and products suitable for home care.

5. How do I book an appointment at Nail Salon Malvern?

You can book appointments online by visiting our website https://nailsalonmalvern.com/. Alternatively, you can call us directly at (484) 26-5290 to schedule your visit.
