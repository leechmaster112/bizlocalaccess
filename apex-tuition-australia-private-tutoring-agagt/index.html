Frequently Asked Questions

What subjects and year levels do you tutor?

We offer comprehensive tutoring across all major school subjects including English, Mathematics (including VCE Maths Methods, Specialist, Further, Business Studies, Accounting, Science, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, History, Geography, Economics, and Humanities. Our services cater to students from primary school through to VCE and beyond.

Do you only offer tutoring in the Carlton VIC area?

While our registered office is in Carlton, VIC, our tutoring services are primarily focused on providing support to students in the Melbourne metropolitan area, including online tutoring nationwide. Our online platform allows us to reach students across Australia and internationally.

How does the online tutoring work?

Our online tutoring uses secure, interactive platforms designed for effective learning. Tutors join scheduled virtual classrooms, share screens, use interactive tools, and communicate clearly with students to deliver engaging lessons. We ensure a stable and supportive online learning environment.

How do I book a session?

Booking is simple. You can contact our friendly team via phone at 0405 532 487 or through our website https://www.apextuitionaustralia.com/. We will discuss your child's needs, arrange a suitable tutor, and schedule regular sessions tailored to their timetable.

Are your tutors qualified?

Absolutely. All our tutors are highly qualified professionals, holding relevant teaching qualifications, degrees in their subject areas, or significant industry experience. They undergo a rigorous vetting process and are selected for their expertise, patience, and ability to inspire students.
