Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What exactly does bathtub refinishing involve?

A1: Bathtub refinishing involves stripping the old surface down to the bare substrate, meticulously filling any chips or cracks, applying multiple layers of specialized refinishing compounds (often including a gel or epoxy), and finishing with a durable clear protective coat. This process restores the appearance and provides a new, seamless surface.

Q2: How long does a typical refinishing job take?

A2: A standard bathtub refinishing job typically takes between 1 to 3 business days, depending on the complexity of the job, the condition of the tub, and the specific refinishing product used.
