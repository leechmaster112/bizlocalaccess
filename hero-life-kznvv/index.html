2. How does a Hero Life mobile personal training session work?

It's simple! We start with a virtual or in-person consultation to discuss your goals and needs. Then, our certified trainer visits your chosen location (home, backyard, park, etc.) for your first session. We conduct an initial assessment, explain the customised workout plan, and guide you through your first exercises. Subsequent sessions are scheduled at times that work best for you, with the trainer coming to you equipped with all necessary equipment.

3. Are your personal trainers qualified and experienced?

Absolutely. All Hero Life trainers are highly qualified (holding certifications like Cert IV in Fitness, BPTT, and other relevant qualifications) and are committed to ongoing professional development. Our trainer will advise on any specific equipment needed during your consultation.
