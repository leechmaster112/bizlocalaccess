Common Conditions We Treat Locally

If you are experiencing any of the following issues within your feet, ankles, or legs, you are the right place to seek expert care at our Boca Raton location:

Venous Reflux Disease (Chronic Venous Insufficiency): Characterized by blood pooling in the veins due to faulty valves, leading to swelling (edema), aching pain, heaviness, skin discoloration, and sometimes ulcers. Varicose Veins: Enlarged, twisted veins that are often visible under the skin, typically occurring in the legs and feet. They can be unsightly and cause discomfort. Spider and Telangiectasia Veins: These are smaller, surface-level veins that appear as red or blue web-like patterns or thin lines on the skin. Deep Vein Thrombosis (DVT): A serious condition involving blood clots forming in the deep veins of the leg, requiring prompt medical attention. Arterial Disease: Conditions affecting the arteries in the legs, such as peripheral artery disease (PAD), which can cause pain, numbness, or non-healing wounds. Lymphedema: Swelling caused by a buildup of lymph fluid in the tissues, often following surgery or injury.

Advanced Technology and Minimally Invasive Treatments
