Gearhouse Coffee Shop

(formerly known as Basecamp Coffee Shop) Your friendly, local coffee shop and café in the heart of the PNW. Our guides serve craft lattes featuring locally roasted coffee, draft beer, specialty tea, pastries, and hearty hot breakfast food in a spacious community setting with an outdoorsy mountain vibe. With fast WiFi, comfy chairs, cozy booths, plenty of outlets, and bright natural light, it’s the perfect spot for the outdoorsy local, remote worker, or traveler. Whether you’re stopping by for a seasonal homemade pumpkin spice latte or indulging in our signature chili mocha, the Gearhouse Coffee Shop is more than just a coffee store—it’s your go-to bar, tea store, and gathering place for adventure and connection.
