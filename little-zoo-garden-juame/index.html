Little Zoo Garden

Little Zoo Garden is a family-friendly animal cafe and mini-zoo located in Bangkok, Thailand, where visitors can interact with a variety of exotic and domestic animals in a relaxed setting, including capybaras, goats, rabbits, hedgehogs, dogs like Chihuahuas and corgis, cats, meerkats, deer, wallabies, birds such as cockatiels and cockatoos, tortoises, nutria, ducks, turtles, musk rats, bamboo rats, and more. It's designed as a petting and feeding garden at our capybara cafe, offering hands-on experiences like feeding, petting, and holding animals, making it popular for families, kids, and animal lovers. The venue combines outdoor and indoor areas with a cafe for drinks and snacks, allowing unlimited stay once inside.
