Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What types of tours do you offer in Anchorage?

We specialize in authentic Anchorage city tours, showcasing local history, culture, and hidden gems. We also offer wildlife adventure excursions focusing on local marine life like Dall's Porpoises and harbor seals, often accessible from within the city or its immediate surroundings.

Q2: Can I book a tour for just one person?

Absolutely! We welcome solo travelers. Please contact us using our website or phone number to discuss your preferred tour and availability.

Q3: How far in advance should I book my Anchorage tour?

We recommend booking at least a few days in advance, especially during peak season or for weekend tours. However, last-minute bookings are often possible depending on availability.

Q4: Are your tours suitable for all ages?

Our city tours are generally suitable for all ages. Our wildlife adventures may involve walking on potentially uneven surfaces and require moderate activity levels. Please contact us for specific details on accessibility and physical requirements for each tour.

Q5: What should I bring on my Anchorage tour?

It's best to dress in layers appropriate for Anchorage weather, even during summer months. Bring comfortable walking shoes, a camera, sunscreen, and a reusable water bottle. We provide essential information about specific packing requirements closer to your tour date.
