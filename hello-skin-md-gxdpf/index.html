Frequently Asked Questions

1. What types of skin conditions do you treat at your Phoenix location?

At Hello Skin MD in Phoenix, we treat a wide range of common and complex skin conditions. This includes acne and its variants, rosacea, eczema, psoriasis, hyperpigmentation (like dark spots), melasma, skin allergies, fungal infections, warts, precancerous lesions, and concerns related to aging skin. Our focus is on personalized diagnosis and treatment.

2. Do I need a referral to see a dermatologist at Hello Skin MD?

No, you do not need a referral to schedule an appointment with a dermatologist at Hello Skin MD. We welcome patients seeking dermatological care directly. While referrals are sometimes obtained from other physicians, they are not required for initial consultations at our Phoenix practice.

3. What should I expect during my first visit to Hello Skin MD?

Your first visit to Hello Skin MD will involve an initial consultation. Our staff will gather your medical history, including any past skin issues or relevant health information. During the appointment, our dermatologist will perform a thorough evaluation, possibly using our data-backed diagnostic tools. We will discuss findings, answer your questions, and recommend a treatment plan tailored to your needs. We may also provide guidance on your home skincare routine.

4. How long does a typical skin consultation take?

A standard initial skin consultation at Hello Skin MD typically lasts about 45-60 minutes. This allows ample time for a comprehensive evaluation, discussion of your concerns, and development of your personalized treatment plan. Follow-up appointments may vary depending on the complexity of your case and the treatments discussed.

5. Are the treatments at Hello Skin MD painful?

We prioritize patient comfort. While some treatments may involve a brief sensation (like a slight warmth from laser or a mild tingling from chemical peels), our team uses advanced techniques and settings tailored to minimize discomfort. We offer topical numbing creams for many procedures if needed. We will discuss any potential sensations and ensure you feel comfortable throughout your visit to our Phoenix location.
